## Findings

### Finding 1: A/B qot Positional INVERSION — A Is LF-Enriched, B Is LF-Depleted/MID-Enriched

A-dialect qot is LF-enriched (E=1.65×, N=72 at LF), depleted at MID_inner (E=0.81×) and LL (E=0.72×). B-dialect qot is LF-DEPLETED (E=0.51×, N=47 at LF — well below its token frequency of 4.0%) and MID/pre_LL enriched (E=1.14×/1.27×). This is a complete positional inversion between dialects. Analogous to daiin: B daiin=LF-formula (E=2.57×), A daiin=LL-content (E=1.37×). qot's inversion is: A qot=LF cluster, B qot=MID/pre_LL cluster.

### Finding 2: A qot Self-Succession Is LF+MID with Complete Absence at pre_LL→LL (E=0.00×, N=0/36)

A-dialect qot→qot: LF→pos1 E=3.34× (N=6/72), MID_inner E=3.00× (N=8/107), pre_LL→LL E=0.00× (0/36). qot-class words cluster at LF and MID positions but completely fail to sustain into LL. This means A-dialect qot succession chains open and develop in the line body but do not reach the LL position. The LL "block" on qot self-succession is the strongest such constraint found so far (E=0.00× with N=36 is statistically robust). Architecture: A qot onset-class memory rule applies only at LF/MID positions.

### Finding 3: B qot Self-Succession Is MID-Concentrated (E=2.30×) Consistent With qot Being a B MID Class

B-dialect qot→qot overall E=2.06× (N=56/634). Per position: LF→pos1=1.49×, MID_inner=2.30× (N=47/477), pre_LL→LL=1.27×. The MID concentration (2.30×) is the highest E, consistent with B qot being a MID/pre_LL class (positional distribution finding). B qot clusters in the middle of lines where it's most frequent.

### Finding 4: A qot → ch at LF→pos1 (E=1.77×, N=28/72) and A/B Cross-Dialect Consistency

A-dialect qot→ch at LF→pos1 E=1.77× (28/72 = 38.9% ch at pos1 after qot at LF). This is a LF formula coupling: A qot-class word at LF → ch-class word at pos1. Compare: A qot→ch at MID=0.81× (depleted). B-dialect qot→ch at LF→pos1=1.72× (14/47) — same LF→pos1 coupling, cross-dialect consistent. qot at LF → ch at pos1 is a cross-dialect rule: when qot appears at LF (common in A, rare in B), ch follows at pos1.

### Finding 5: A qot→y (MID E=2.86×, N=14/107) and A qot→qok (MID E=2.29×, N=14/107) Are MID-Succession Rules

A-dialect qot→y: MID_inner E=2.86× (14/107), LF→pos1=0.91× (near baseline), pre_LL→LL=0.00× (absent). qot→y in A is a PURE MID SUCCESSION rule — operates only in MID positions, not at line start or end. No dominant word pairs (all N=1). Similarly, A qot→qok: MID_inner E=2.29× (14/107), LF=0.73×, pre_LL=0.49×. Both y and qok are preferred successors of qot IN THE MIDDLE OF A-DIALECT LINES.

### Finding 6: A qot→daiin Is Pre_LL→LL-Concentrated (E=2.28×, N=12/36) — Confirming F366

A-dialect qot→d overall E=1.02× (near baseline) due to LF/MID near-baseline (0.76×/0.77×). But **pre_LL→LL E=2.28×** (N=12/36): when A-dialect qot appears at pre_LL position, daiin appears at LL at 33.3% (vs 14.6% baseline). Top pairs: qoteey→daiin(3), qotchey→daiin(2), qotor→daiin(2), qotol→daiin(2). This confirms F366's A qot→daiin pre_LL E=2.52×. Architecture: A qot is a line-terminal predecessor of daiin (qot at pre_LL → daiin at LL formula).

### Finding 7: B qot→r Is Pre_LL→LL-Concentrated (E=5.18×, N=9/110) — New LL Rule

B-dialect qot→r: overall E=2.00× (exp_367), BUT this decomposes to pre_LL→LL E=5.18× (N=9/110) + LF=1.35× + MID=1.33× (near-moderate). The dominant mechanism is qot at pre_LL → r-onset word at LL. Top pairs: qoteoly→raiin, qotedy→raiin, qotedy→rchr. r-onset LL words after B qot: raiin (2), rchr (1), rorarol (1), rchedy (1) — diverse r-onset LL forms. This is a B-dialect LINE-TERMINAL rule: B qot at pre_LL forecasts r-onset at LL.

### Finding 8: B qot→d Is Also Pre_LL-Concentrated (E=2.53×, N=15/110) — B qot Is a "LL-Predictor"

B qot→d: pre_LL→LL E=2.53× (15/110), MID=1.17×, LF=0.00×. Both B qot→r AND B qot→d are strongly pre_LL→LL concentrated. B qot at pre_LL predicts LL terminals (r-onset OR d-onset). Combined: (9 r + 15 d) / 110 = 21.8% of B qot pre_LL → r-or-d at LL (vs baseline ~7%). B qot is a "pre-LL terminal predictor" class — when B qot appears near line-end, the line terminates with r or d onset words.

### Finding 9: B qot→ot Is MID-Concentrated (E=1.72×, N=59/477) — ot Template-Pair

B-dialect qot→ot: MID E=1.72× (59/477), pre_LL=1.52×, LF=0.59×. B qot→ot is a MID succession rule (like B qot self-succession). Top pairs: qotedy→otedy(4), qotain→oteedy(4), qotar→otar(2) — body-parallel pairs (qot+ar → ot+ar, same body different onset). This is body-parallel templating: the body class (ar/ain/edy) persists across onset change.
