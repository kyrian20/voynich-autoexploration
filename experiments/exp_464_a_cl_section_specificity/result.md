## Interpretation

**H_new_AB CONFIRMED: A cross-line grammar is section-specific, but in a different pattern than B.**

### A-dialect cross-line section taxonomy

**herbal_a (f1-56, N=673 CL pairs):**
- ch→ch: 1.5×(N=22) — confirmed within-section
- ok→y: 1.7×(N=11)
- s→qot: 3.2×(N=5) — primary locus
- s→s: 3.0×(N=4)
- sh→d: 1.9×(N=8)

**cosmo (f85-102, N=234 CL pairs):**
- ch→ch: 2.2×(N=6) — stronger than herbal_a, but N=6
- ok→y: 1.8×(N=5)
- (s→qot and s→s: effectively absent in cosmo)

**stars (f57-72, N=86 CL pairs):**
- ALL cross-line couplings: near-zero or 0. No elevated coupling in any pair.
- This is the A-dialect "astronomical/zodiac" section — completely different from B astro (which shows ch→qot CL 1.9×)

**Three structural differences between A and B section grammar:**

1. **A ch→ch CL is CROSS-SECTION (herbal_a + cosmo both elevated)**; B ch→ch is not a finding (F442 is ch→qot, an A→B inversion). The A "ch self-succession" grammar is distributed across both major A sections, not section-specific.

2. **A s→qot CL is herbal_a-concentrated** (5 of 6 cases); B has no s→qot CL (B s secondary target is qok, not qot — F443).

3. **A "stars" section (astro equivalent) shows ZERO cross-line couplings** — completely different from B astro which has ch→qot CL (F442, F444). This is a strong A/B asymmetry at the section level.

4. **A d→sh CL is WEAK (1.13× overall)** vs B 1.78× — the herbal/pharma sh-d alternating pattern is a B-specific phenomenon, not A (A herbal_a d→sh=1.3×, not significant).

5. **A shows less section differentiation than B**: ch→ch and ok→y both appear in herbal_a AND cosmo. B's section grammar has clearer specialization (astro≠herbal≠pharma).

## Self-Audit

**Target-awareness**: No target-aware elements. Pairs pre-specified from existing findings (F442/F443/F444 targets), not selected post-hoc from results. The matrix output includes all top LL onsets without filtering. No parameter calibrated to any VMS metric.

**Feature-destroying implementation**: No feature-destroying details. Implementation is identical to exp_462 (confirmed to work for B). The section classifier for A uses folio number ranges matching manuscript section boundaries.

**Extrapolation**: The "stars section shows all-zeros" result needs qualification. Stars N=86 CL pairs. With N=86, detecting a coupling at E=1.5× would require ~3 cases (base_p~0.04 for ch→ch → expected 3.4). Multiple pairs all showing 0 cases is consistent with genuine absence, but cannot rule out E=1.3× effects at N=86. Claim softened: "No elevated cross-line coupling detected in A stars section at N=86 CL pairs." Not claiming "stars cannot have cross-line grammar."

**Negative-result scope**: The A d→sh CL weakness (1.13× overall) is a comparative negative vs B (1.78×). This is within-experiment scope: "A d→sh CL is weaker than B d→sh CL and not section-concentrated in A herbal_a." Not claimed that A d→sh is zero — 1.13× with N=24 could be true signal at low magnitude.

**Control behavior**: No explicit shuffle control. The baseline is section-specific LF onset distribution (same control as exp_462). E computed as obs / (section_LF_base_rate × n_ll). Section base rates computed from same corpus, same section. No control/real data comparison here — purely descriptive section breakdown of the CL onset coupling pattern.

## Conclusion

**H_new_AB CONFIRMED: A cross-line grammar is section-specific.** Key findings:
1. herbal_a concentrates: s→qot(3.2×), s→s(3.0×), sh→d(1.9×), ch→ch(1.5×), ok→y(1.7×)
2. cosmo shares: ch→ch(2.2×), ok→y(1.8×) — less section-differentiated than B
3. A "stars" section has ZERO cross-line coupling elevation — opposite of B astro which drives ch→qot CL
4. A d→sh is WEAK (1.13× overall, 1.3× in herbal_a) — B herbal/pharma d→sh alternation is B-specific
5. NEW directional: cth→sh CL: E=1.9×(N=12) — previously unexamined

**No new finding ID** (results refine A section structure; N values adequate for herbal_a main pairs but section-specific cells borderline). **F445 candidate**: A cross-line section grammar taxonomy (herbal_a: s→qot, s→s, sh→d, ch→ch, ok→y; cosmo: ch→ch, ok→y shared).
