## Interpretation

**H_new_CK ANSWERED: A herbal_a has TWO confirmed pen rules + multiple strong directionals.**

The pen grammar of A herbal_a is significantly richer than previously characterized. Before this experiment, only F474 (cth-LF→d@pen) was confirmed. Now:

1. **F485 NEW CONFIRMED:** ok-LF → ok-self@pen (E=2.73×/N=12). A herbal_a's second most common LF class has a confirmed self-chain at pen.

2. **F474 REPRODUCED:** cth-LF → d@pen (E=1.70×/N=18) — slight E increase from prior 1.61×, consistent.

3. **Near-threshold directionals for ot-LF:** ot→cth@pen (E=3.58×/N=9) and ot-self@pen (E=3.51×/N=8) are one observation short of confirmation. These would be the strongest confirmed pen rules in A herbal_a if confirmed. A dedicated ot-LF pen scope test using all A herbal_a ot-LF lines from the full corpus might push these over threshold.

4. **ok/a@pen cross-dialect signal:** ok-LF → a@pen is dir in A herbal_a (E=3.18×/N=6), paralleling CONF in B dialect (F459). This suggests a universal ok-LF → a@pen tendency with A herbal_a also having ok-self@pen as a distinct (possibly competing) signal.

**A herbal_a pen grammar summary:**
- CONF: ok-self@pen (F485), d@pen for cth-LF (F474)
- Near-CONF (dir): ot-LF → cth@pen (N=9), ot-self@pen (N=8), ok→a@pen (N=6), a-LF → o@pen (N=6)
- Classes with flat pen: d-LF (well-powered negative, N_elig=261), ch-LF (N_elig=186)
