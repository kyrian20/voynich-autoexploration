# exp_350: Bio Section sh+ckhy Compounds — Positional and Section Distribution (H552)

**Date:** 2026-04-11
**Hypothesis:** H552 — shckhy and sheckhy are bio-specific structural tokens. Are they also section-specific and what are their positional properties?
**Result:** H552 ANSWERED. sh+ckhy compounds (shckhy/sheckhy) are overwhelmingly MID-position (96-97%) and are bio+cosmo concentrated (shckhy: bio=52%, cosmo=40%; sheckhy: bio=61%, cosmo=32%). They are NOT bio-exclusive — cosmo has nearly as many. The parallel ch+ckhy forms (chckhy=122, checkhy=46) are much more frequent and spread across cosmo+bio+herbal. ALL ckhy compounds are >85% MID with near-zero LF (0-2%). The bio section's higher sh+ckhy rate (per sh-onset token) vs ch-dominant folios is confirmed, but the absolute tokens are comparable between bio and cosmo.

## Findings

### Finding 1: sh+ckhy Compounds Are Pure MID Words (0% LF)

All sh+ckhy forms (shckhy, sheckhy, shecthy) have 0% LF. They never initiate a line. With 96-97% MID rate, these are pure content/grammar words that occupy interior positions only. This is consistent with the sh-onset being a template-class onset that avoids folio-first and folio-last LF positions.

### Finding 2: sh+ckhy Is bio+cosmo Concentrated, Not Bio-Exclusive

shckhy: bio=27 (52%), cosmo=21 (40%), herbal=2 (4%). sheckhy: bio=19, cosmo=10. Together they appear in both bio and cosmo sections. The F350 finding (shckhy enriched 8.94× in bio vs ch-dominant folios) was comparing bio to the ch-dominant subset of cosmo — the ch-dominant cosmo folios (f104-115) have fewer shckhy per sh-onset word than bio, but cosmo overall has many shckhy words. The rate per sh token is higher in bio than cosmo.

### Finding 3: ch+ckhy Forms Are More Frequent and More Widespread

chckhy (N=122, ch-parallel of shckhy) appears in cosmo=38, bio=37, herbal=36 — spread across three major sections. checkhy (N=46): bio=21, cosmo=16, herbal=7. The ch+ckhy forms are ~2× as frequent as sh+ckhy forms AND appear in herbal (sh+ckhy is nearly absent from herbal). This asymmetry means ch+ckhy is the primary ckhy compound class; sh+ckhy is a bio+cosmo-restricted complement.

### Finding 4: The ckhy Body Class Forms a Structural Compound in VMS Grammar

The ckhy body element appears in compound structures: sh+ckhy, ch+ckhy. These compounds are pure MID words. The ckhy compound is not section-specific — it appears across bio, cosmo, and herbal, but the sh vs ch CARRIER differs by section (herbal: ch-carrier preferred; bio/cosmo: both sh and ch carriers). This is consistent with F350's finding that bio enforces ch/sh symmetry — the ckhy compound slot is accessible to BOTH sh and ch in bio, while in herbal only ch accesses the ckhy slot.

## Summary

H552 ANSWERED. The bio sh+ckhy compounds reflect bio's ch/sh SYMMETRY (F350): in bio, BOTH ch and sh can fill the template-carrier role before the ckhy body. In herbal, only ch carries ckhy (chckhy=36, shckhy=2). The bio section's distinctive sh+ckhy rate (per sh token) vs ch-dominant folios is real but cosmologically the absolute token counts are comparable to cosmo. The ckhy compound class is a pure MID word type (96-97% MID) appearing across bio/cosmo/herbal but with sh+carrier restricted to bio/cosmo and ch+carrier widespread.
