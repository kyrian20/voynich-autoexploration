## Interpretation

**H_new_Z CONFIRMED: B cross-line grammar is section-specific.**

**Section-specific CL grammar patterns:**

1. **ch→qot CL is ASTRO-concentrated**: 6/9 overall cases from astro (E=1.9× in astro). This links F442 to astro section content. Within-line astro grammar features elevated qok-LF (F431). The ch→qot cross-line coupling may reflect: astro paragraphs where ch-terminal lines precede qot-opening lines specifically. ch→qot in herbal/pharma is at baseline.

2. **d→sh CL is HERBAL + PHARMA** (not astro): herbal E=2.3×(N=5), pharma E=1.8×(N=4), astro only 1.2×. The d→sh cross-line rule operates in the botanical/pharmaceutical content domain. Within-line herbal grammar features d→ch→y formula (F430). d→sh CL may be a paragraph-level complement: d-terminal lines in herbal/pharma precede sh-opening lines.

3. **ok→y CL is HERBAL + ASTRO** (not pharma): herbal=2.1×(N=10), astro=2.1×(N=4), pharma=1.0×(N=7) near-baseline. ok→y CL is completely absent from pharma despite having 7 events (baseline rate).

4. **qok→s CL is PHARMA-elevated**: pharma E=1.7×(N=13), astro only 1.2×(N=16) despite 52% of cases coming from astro. The qok→s coupling is a PHARMA grammar rule. In pharma, qok-terminal lines predict s-opening at next line. In astro, qok→s is near-baseline (astro's dominant cross-line routing is d→qok and ch→qot, not qok→s).

5. **d→qok CL is ASTRO-DOMINATED by count** (20/26 from astro) but only 1.4× within astro. The overall d→qok E=1.56× is partly a section-composition artifact (d in astro lines precede qok-LF lines because astro globally has elevated qok-LF). Herbal and pharma show genuine d→qok elevation (1.5-1.8×).

6. **sh→d CL is HERBAL + PHARMA**: herbal E=2.1×(N=5), pharma E=1.6×(N=7). Same section pattern as d→sh CL — both are herbal+pharma couplings.

**Cross-line section grammar taxonomy (B-dialect):**
- **Astro**: ch→qot CL (2.36×, F442; driven by astro); d→qok by count (composition effect)
- **Herbal**: ok→y CL (2.1×); d→sh CL (2.3×); sh→d CL (2.1×) — sh/d/ok alternating patterns
- **Pharma**: qok→s CL (1.7×); d→sh CL (1.8×); sh→d CL (1.6×) — s and d-sh patterns
- **Herbal+Astro**: ok→y (shared cross-section)
- **Herbal+Pharma**: d→sh + sh→d (botanical/pharmaceutical shared)

This section taxonomy parallels but differs from within-line section grammar (F430):
- Within-line astro: qok-LF elevated (F431) → cross-line astro: ch→qot CL
- Within-line herbal: d→ch→y / y→sh→ch formulas (F430) → cross-line herbal: d→sh / ok→y CL
- Within-line pharma: l-LF class (F430) → cross-line pharma: qok→s CL
