## H151: daiin-B Is NOT a Cross-Line Connector from ch/sh Endings — Preceded by o-Onset Lines

### Preceding Line's Last Word Onset (When daiin Opens Current B Line)

| Onset | daiin-prev% | non-daiin-prev% | Enrichment |

| **o** | **35.2%** | 27.7% | **1.27×** |

The hypothesis that daiin-B opening a line follows lines ending with ch/sh is **REFUTED**. When daiin opens a B line, the preceding line ends with **o-onset** most often (35.2% vs background 27.7%). **ch-onset is SUPPRESSED** (0.83×), not enriched, before daiin-opening lines.

### 3-Line Pattern (prev_last onset → daiin[0] → curr_last onset)

| Pattern | Count | % |
|---------|-------|---|
| [o] → daiin → [o] | 13 | **14.8%** |

| [qo] → daiin → [other] | 5 | 5.7% |
| [o] → daiin → [ch] | 4 | 4.5% |

The dominant 3-line pattern is [o-ending line] → [daiin-opening line] → [o-ending line]. daiin-B follows o-onset endings, not ch/sh. The connector hypothesis for daiin-B was wrong — daiin does not bridge ch/sh content blocks.

### Revised Interpretation: daiin-B as o-Continuation Marker

daiin-B line-openers follow lines ending with o-onset words (35.2%), and daiin-opened lines ALSO tend to end with o-onset words (31.8%). The 3-line unit is [o-ending] → [daiin + ch/sh opener] → [o-ending]. daiin-B may be a LINE-INITIATING PARTICLE that marks a continuation of the o-onset content register, while internally introducing ch/sh-onset words on its line.

### H151 Verdict: REFUTED — daiin-B Is NOT a ch/sh Cross-Line Connector; Preceding Lines End with o-Onset (35.2%, 1.27×), Not ch/sh; ch-Onset Preceding Is Suppressed (0.83×)

This is a SELECTIONAL constraint, not random section preference. The section-level difference (oly in cosmo, -am in herbal) follows from section vocabulary preferences for {ch/qo} vs {s/d/sh} onset words.

### H152 Verdict: SUPPORTED — oly/oldy and -am Select Different Preceding Onset Classes (oly→s/d/sh; -am→ch/qo); Selection Is Content-Driven, Not Random

## Combined Finding (F095)

**H151 REFUTED**: daiin-B does NOT follow ch/sh-ending lines. Preceding line when daiin opens B: o-onset=35.2% (1.27×), ch=11.4% (0.83× SUPPRESSED). 3-line pattern: [o-ending] → [daiin-B] → [o-ending] (14.8%). daiin-B connector function operates within o-onset content context, not ch/sh.

**H152 SUPPORTED**: oly/oldy and -am have different preceding-onset selectional requirements. oly/oldy preceded by s-onset (6.88×), d-onset (3.06×), sh-onset (2.29×). -am preceded by ch-onset (0.57× for oly) and qo-onset (0.76×). The perfect complementary distribution (H149) is thus a selectional consequence — each class closes lines from its preferred preceding onset class.

**H153 STRONGLY SUPPORTED**: y-compound prefix (y+k/t+suffix) is a LINE-INITIAL MORPHOLOGICAL SYSTEM — LFirst=47.3% in B (3.75× background), 40.3% in A (2.24×). Contrasts sharply with qo-prefix (LFirst=11.3%, at background). Both select k/t stems but occupy different line positions. **Full B line-position grammar**: [y-compound/s-onset LINE-OPENER] → [qo-prefix MID-LINE] → [-am/oly LINE-CLOSER], with daiin-B as LINE-CONNECTOR following o-ending lines.
