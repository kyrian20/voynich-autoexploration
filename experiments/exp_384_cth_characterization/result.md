## Findings

### Finding 1: A cth Is LF=0.13× — The MOST LF-DEPLETED of All Characterized Onset Classes; Not a Second-Position Class

A cth: LF=0.13×(N=8/1491), pos1=1.28×, MID=1.00×, pre_LL=1.25×, LL=1.42×. A cth is the most LF-depleted class characterized (A ch=0.39×, B ch=0.25×, B sh: not strongly LF-depleted). A cth almost never appears at line-first position. Unlike ch/sh (which are LF-depleted because they are "second-position response classes" concentrated at pos1), A cth is NOT specifically pos1-enriched (pos1=1.28×, similar to pre_LL=1.25× and LL=1.42×) — it is simply absent from LF and distributed across all other positions roughly equally. Architecture: A cth is a "mid-to-end" class: it appears in the body and end of lines, never at the opening, and has no strong concentration in any specific non-LF position.

### Finding 2: A cth→ot pre_LL=3.03× (N=8/60) — New A LL Routing Rule; cth at pre_LL Routes to ot at LL

A cth at pre_LL → ot at LL: E=2.33×(N=8/60) from Section 7; from Section 4: A cth→ot pre_LL=3.03×(N=8/60). Both measurements consistent. Top pairs: cthor→otol(2), cthor→oty(2). A cth→ot is the dominant ot-enriched coupling at pre_LL. This is a NEW A-dialect LL routing rule. Updated A LL routing: {qot,qok,ok,ot}→daiin (F380/F379/F378); ch→r pre_LL (F383); **cth→ot pre_LL (E=2.33-3.03×, NEW)**. A cth also shows A cth pre_LL → qok LL E=1.96×(N=4/60, moderate) and A cth pre_LL → ok LL=0.29×(depleted). Architecture: A cth at pre_LL preferentially produces ot-class LL words; the qok/ok distinction (ok depleted, qok mildly elevated) suggests cth routing avoids ok-onset LL.

### Finding 3: A cth→r=0.00× (Categorical, N=0/251) — Continues A r-Exclusion Pattern

A cth→r: 0 occurrences out of 251 A cth-outgoing bigrams. Categorical absence. This extends the A-dialect r-exclusion pattern: A sh→r=0.00×(F384), A y→r=0.00×(F387), A cth→r=0.00×(NEW). Together with A d→r=0.38×(near-categorical, F385), the A-dialect systematic grammar excludes r-onset succession from d/y/sh/cth classes. Only A ch→r at pre_LL (E=6.39×, F383) produces r.
