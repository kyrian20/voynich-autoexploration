## Findings

### Finding 1: A ok Is MID-Enriched (E=1.14×), Not LL-Enriched — ok Does NOT Form a qok/ok Spatial Pair Analogous to qot/ot

A-dialect ok positional: LF=0.81×, MID=1.14×, LL=0.96× (near baseline). The ot/qot mirror-image spatial pair (qot=LF cluster, ot=LL cluster) does NOT have a qok/ok analogue. ok is a MID-enriched class, neither distinctively LF nor LL. H589 prediction (ok LL-enriched = spatial pair with qok) is REFUTED. The qot/ot complementarity is specific to those two classes in A-dialect.

### Finding 2: A ok→qok MID E=1.68× — Parallel to A qot→qok (MID E=2.29×)

A ok→qok: MID_inner=1.68× (N=27/282), LF=0.71×, pre_LL=1.28×. Compare A qot→qok: MID=2.29×, LF=0.73× (both depleted at LF). Both qot and ok in A-dialect MID → qok successor. This is a GENERALIZED A-dialect MID template-to-qok rule: {qot, ok} when in MID position → elevated qok successor. Architecture: A-dialect qok is a successor class for multiple template classes in MID positions.

### Finding 3: B ok→ol pre_LL→LL E=2.00× (N=18/161) + B ok→d pre_LL→LL E=1.96× — Parallel to B ot→ol (1.68×) and B ot→d (1.26×)

B ok→ol pre_LL→LL E=2.00× (18/161). Compare B ot→ol pre_LL=1.68× (18/192). The rule **{ok, ot} pre_LL → ol at LL** is cross-class in B-dialect. Similarly, B ok→d pre_LL=1.96× parallels B ot→d pre_LL=1.26×. The generalization: **B-dialect {ok, ot} at pre_LL position → d or ol at LL**. Both these template classes forecast specific LL terminals when near line-end.

### Finding 4: A ok and ot Are SEPARATE Succession Chains (ok→ot depleted E=0.62×)

A ok→ot E=0.62× (N=12/438, depleted). Both ok→ok (E=1.81×) and ot→ot (E=1.92×) are elevated, but ok→ot is depleted. ok and ot each form their OWN succession chains — they do not mix. Similarly check ot→ok from exp_374: A ot→ok=1.21× (moderately elevated), but A ok→ot=0.62× (depleted) — the relationship is ASYMMETRIC: ot can follow ok (1.21×) but ok avoids following ot (0.62×). Architecture: ok and ot are parallel but non-interchangeable succession classes.

### Finding 5: B ok→sh at pre_LL→LL Is Strongly Depleted (E=0.13×, N=2/161)

B ok→sh at pre_LL→LL: E=0.13× (N=2/161). sh is almost completely absent at LL after B ok at pre_LL. Compare B ot→sh pre_LL=0.16× (F378) — both B {ok, ot} at pre_LL strongly avoid sh at LL. Generalization: B template classes {ok, ot} at pre_LL → NOT sh at LL. Combined with B {ok,ot} pre_LL → ol/d at LL (findings 3): the B pre_LL→LL transition is specifically selected toward {d, ol} and away from {sh, qot} when the pre_LL word is ok or ot.

### Finding 6: A ok→ch LF→pos1 E=1.54×, Parallel to A qot→ch LF→pos1 (E=1.77×)

A ok→ch at LF→pos1 E=1.54× (25/74). This parallels A qot→ch at LF→pos1 (E=1.77×) from F377. Both qot and ok at LF → ch at pos1 in A-dialect. A GENERALIZED A-dialect LF formula: when qot or ok appears at LF (rare for ok, since LF=0.81×, more common for qot at 1.65×), ch follows at pos1. Note: from exp_373, B qot→ch LF→pos1=1.72× also. This cross-dialect LF→ch rule now extends to ok as well.

### Finding 7: ok Body Structure Is Distinct From ot/qot — More 'eol'/'eey' in A; More 'aiin' in B

A ok top bodies: ol(7.4%), aiin(7.0%), eol(6.8%), eey(6.6%), al(6.6%). The 'eol' (6.8%) and 'eey' (6.6%) bodies are prominent in A ok but not A ot (ot top: ol=8.6%, aiin=7.8%, y=7.8%). B ok top body: aiin(12.4%) dominates — much higher concentration than B ot aiin(8.3%) or B qot aiin(8.7%). B ok is the most aiin-body-concentrated onset class among {qot, ot, ok}.
