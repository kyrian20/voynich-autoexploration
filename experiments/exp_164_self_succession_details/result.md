## H330: qot→qot SPECIFIC PAIRS (A-dialect, n=15)

This is consistent with qok-words preceding the B-type l-vocabulary in late A-herbal sections.

## H333: ch_e↔r BIDIRECTIONAL PAIRS (A-dialect)

**ch_e→r (n=6)**: All 6 word pairs are different:
- chey<->ctho,d → ram; cheo → r; chey → r; cheo → ro; cheol → raiin; cheol → rar
- Diverse ch_e-words (chey, cheo, cheol) before diverse r-words (r, ro, raiin, rar, ram)

**r→ch_e (n=6)**: All 6 word pairs:
- r → cheey (×2); r → chear,een; r → cheeey; r → cheody; r → chey
- All from bare `r` word before diverse ch_e-words. `r→cheey` appears 2×.

**The pairs are NOT the same words reversed**: ch_e→r uses chey/cheo/cheol before diverse r-words; r→ch_e uses only bare `r` before ch_e-words. This is asymmetric — `r` (bare minimal form) specifically precedes ch_e words (2.55×), and ch_e words precede diverse r-forms. Not a fixed bidirectional couplet.

**Interpretation**: The bare `r` token in Voynichese functions like a short linking word that specifically precedes ch_e-onset words. The ch_e→r direction shows ch_e words followed by r-forms (also linking behavior). This may reflect a specific syntactic position for `r` and ch_e-words in A-dialect.
