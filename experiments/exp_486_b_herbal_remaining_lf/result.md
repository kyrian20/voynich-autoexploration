## 6. Extended A herbal_a pos2 Self-Chaining Pattern

With F463, F464, and F461, a clear pattern emerges:

| LF class | pos2 onset | E × | N | Status |
|----------|-----------|-----|---|--------|
| ch | ch-self | 1.60× | 94 | CONFIRMED (F461) |
| sh | sh-self | 2.64× | 12 | CONFIRMED (F463) |
| o | o-self | 7.03× | 10 | CONFIRMED (F464) |
| y | ok | 2.25× | 20 | CONFIRMED (F448, different onset) |
| ok | ok-self | dir | N=7 | directional |
| qot | qot-self | 6.48× | 6 | directional |
| s | s-self | 42×(!) | 4 | directional (small N) |

**Pattern: At pos2, LF classes overwhelmingly show their OWN onset class.** ch, sh, o, (qot), (s) self-chain at pos2. y is the exception — it routes to ok (F448). ok routes to ok-self (directional). d-LF is the other exception: d at pos2 is 1.76× (below threshold); the dominant pos2 in d-LF lines is ch (E≈0.99×, flat).

This suggests **pos2 in A herbal_a encodes the line's onset-class signature**: whatever onset terminates the line (LF) typically also appears second in the line. This is a systematic positional grammar rule.
