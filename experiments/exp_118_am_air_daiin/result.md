## H231: CONFIRMED — daiin Is A-Dialect Dominant (45.87‰ A vs 13.05‰ B; 3.5× A-Enriched); Herbal=42.71‰ Highest; LINE-FIRST Enriched 1.60×; After-daiin cth-Onset Enriched 4.89×; daiin Is a Pre-Nominal Determiner Function Word

### Positional Profile

| Position | daiin% | baseline% | ratio |

daiin is enriched at BOTH line-first and line-last positions (unlike typical content words which are mid-line dominant). It's a function word that appears throughout the line, with mild preference for edges.

Compare to other d-onset words:
- dain: first=26.2% (strong line-first bias)

### Bigram Context: What Comes After daiin?

**Words after daiin** (top): chey (11), daiin (10), cthy (9), dain (9), chedy (9), shey (9)...

**Onset class AFTER daiin**:

| Onset | after% | baseline% | ratio |

**daiin is most often followed by cth-onset words (4.89× enrichment)**. cth- is the extreme A-paradigm onset class (F116, F113). This is a strong syntactic association: daiin → cth- pattern suggests daiin functions as a **pre-cth-nominal particle or determiner**.

### Words Before daiin

Most common: chol (31), daiin (10), chedy (10), qokeey (10), shol (9), chor (9)...

**ch-onset appears before daiin at 1.53×** enrichment. ch-onset words (ch + suffix) are the most common words in the text, so this partial enrichment suggests ch-words can precede daiin.

### Same-Line Co-occurrence

Most enriched on daiin-lines: **cthy (2.10×)** — consistent with the after-daiin cth- enrichment finding. chol (1.41×), chor (1.51×), chy (1.45×) also enriched on daiin lines.

### Line Length with daiin

- Lines WITH daiin: mean 8.47 words
- Lines WITHOUT daiin: mean 9.11 words

daiin-lines are 0.64 words shorter on average. This makes sense if daiin occupies a "function word slot" that displaces content.

### Interpretation: daiin as Pre-Nominal Determiner

The evidence consistently points to **daiin as a function word that precedes nominal content words**, especially cth-onset nouns:

1. A-dialect dominant (3.5× A-enriched) — consistent with cth- being A-paradigm
2. After-daiin cth-onset enriched 4.89× — strongest directional bigram signal found
3. Same-line cthy enriched 2.10× — whole-line association with cth- words
4. Balanced positional profile (not strongly first or last) — function words appear throughout the line
5. Appears in 41% of paragraphs ≥3 times — extremely common, consistent with grammatical role

daiin may function as a **definite article or demonstrative** that precedes cth-nominal forms (cth- = "those/these" stems?). The d-onset prefix on daiin itself is consistent with d-onset being A-paradigm morphological (d-onset words are A-dialect line-openers from F117).
