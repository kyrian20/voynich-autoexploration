# Experiment exp_054: 'y-' Prefix Paragraph-Start Analysis
Date: 2026-04-09

## Question (H061, H062)
Is the 'y-' initial line-enrichment concentrated at PARAGRAPH starts (@P0) or at within-paragraph line starts (+P0)? And is -ain a paragraph-boundary marker?

## Results

### 'y-' Initial Words: Paragraph Start vs Continuation Lines

| Position | Currier A y-initial% | Currier B y-initial% |
|----------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Paragraph-start (@P0) | **0.0%** (0/134) | **3.8%** (5/130) |
| Continuation (+P0) | **16.3%** (227/1385) | **14.2%** (343/2416) |
| All lines (baseline) | 14.6% | 13.6% |

**'y-' initial words are COMPLETELY ABSENT from paragraph starts in Currier A** (0/134) and strongly suppressed in Currier B (3.8% vs 14.2% continuation). The 'y-' enrichment at line beginnings seen in exp_053 is entirely a CONTINUATION LINE feature — these words mark within-paragraph line beginnings, NOT paragraph openings.

**Revised interpretation**: 'y-' is a LINE-CONTINUATION PARTICLE — it marks the beginning of a line that continues a sentence/clause from the previous line. It specifically avoids paragraph-initial position.

### Paragraph-Start Markers in Currier A

Suffix distribution at first word of paragraph vs continuation lines:

| Suffix | Para-start% | Continuation% | Ratio |
|--------|------------|--------------|-------|
| **-dy** | **12.7%** | 2.9% | **4.39×** |
| -y | 13.4% | 7.7% | 1.76× |
| -al | 3.0% | 1.9% | 1.59× |
| -iin | 3.7% | 2.2% | 1.67× |
| -or | 14.9% | 16.5% | 0.90 |
| -chy | 1.5% | 5.7% | **0.26** |
| -ain | 0.7% | 3.0% | **0.25** |
| -ey | 1.5% | 4.5% | 0.33 |

**-dy words are 4.39× more likely to start a paragraph than to start a continuation line.** This is the strongest paragraph-start marker found. Meanwhile, -ain (0.25×) and -chy (0.26×) AVOID paragraph starts.

The y-initial words (16.4% of continuation first-words) appear 0% at paragraph starts — the strongest paragraph-start/continuation contrast in the data.

### Paragraph-End Markers

-aiin words at paragraph-final position:
- Currier A: 19.5% of paragraph-end last words end in -aiin (vs 14.4% overall) — **1.35× enriched**
- Currier B: 10.5% of paragraph-end last words end in -aiin (vs 8.1% overall) — **1.30× enriched**

**-aiin marks paragraph endings in both dialects.**

### -ain and -aiin Paragraph-Level Profiles

| Suffix | A para-start first% | A continuation first% | A para-end last% | A all-last% |
|--------|--------------------|-----------------------|-----------------|-------------|
| -ain | 0.7% | 3.0% | 4.7% | 3.3% |
| -aiin | 12.7% | 13.3% | **19.5%** | 14.4% |

-ain is suppressed at paragraph starts and slightly elevated at paragraph ends. -aiin is consistently elevated at paragraph ends in both dialects.

### Summary of Line/Paragraph Positional Grammar in Currier A

| Marker | Position | Enrichment |
|--------|----------|-----------|
| -dy words | **Paragraph-start** (first word) | **4.39×** |
| -y words | Paragraph-start (first word) | 1.76× |
| y-initial words | **Within-paragraph line start** | ~5× vs para-starts |
| -aiin words | **Paragraph-end** (last word) | 1.35× |
| -ain words | Suppressed at paragraph starts | 0.25× |

**The Currier A line/paragraph grammar**:
- **Paragraph opener**: -dy word (4.39× enriched at paragraph starts)
- **Continuation line opener**: y-initial words (16.4% of continuation starts; 0% of para-starts)
- **Paragraph closer**: -aiin words (19.5% of paragraph-end last words)
- **Medial content**: -or, -chy, -ey, -eey words

## Finding (F061)
The 'y-' prefix in Currier A is a LINE-CONTINUATION PARTICLE, not a paragraph-start marker: 0/134 paragraph starts begin with a y-initial word (vs 16.3% of continuation lines). In Currier B, y-initial words are also suppressed at paragraph starts (3.8% vs 14.2%). The actual paragraph-start marker in Currier A is the **-dy suffix** (4.39× enriched at paragraph-start first words vs continuation first words). Paragraph endings are marked by **-aiin** (19.5% of A para-end last words, 10.5% for B). -ain words are suppressed at paragraph starts (0.7% in A, 2.3% in B vs overall rates of 2.9% and 7.8%). This establishes a three-level positional grammar in Currier A: (1) paragraph-start [-dy]; (2) within-paragraph line-start [y-initial, -or]; (3) paragraph-end [-aiin].

## H061 Verdict: NOT SUPPORTED (in original form)
'y-' does NOT mark paragraph starts — it marks LINE-CONTINUATION positions within paragraphs. The actual paragraph-start marker is -dy (4.39×). H062 is PARTIALLY SUPPORTED: -aiin is a paragraph-end marker (1.35× enriched), but -ain shows a more complex pattern (suppressed at para-starts, slightly elevated at para-ends).
