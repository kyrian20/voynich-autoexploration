# Experiment exp_058: f6v Paragraph Structure Analysis
Date: 2026-04-09

## Question (H065)
Is f6v's 38.1% y-initial rate explained by unusually long paragraphs? Or is it a scribal/content anomaly?

## Key Finding: Every Herbal A Folio Is a Single Paragraph

f6v has exactly **1 paragraph** (1 para-start). But this is not anomalous — checking ALL Currier A herbal folios (≥10 lines):

- **75 of 75 herbal A folios have exactly 1 paragraph** (n_paras = 1 for all)
- Every herbal folio is structured as a single continuous block of text

Since all herbal A folios are single paragraphs, **paragraph length cannot explain y-initial rate variation** — the paragraph IS the folio. The "long paragraph" hypothesis is null.

## f6v Is Not an Outlier

| Rank | Folio | y-initial% | Lines |
|------|-------|-----------|-------|
| #1 | f53v | 38.5% | 13 |
| **#2** | **f6v** | **38.1%** | **21** |
| #3 | f19v | 35.7% | 14 |

| #59 | f49r | 0.0% | 21 |

f6v (38.1%) is near the top but not extreme — f53v is higher (38.5%). Many other herbal A folios cluster in the 20-35% range. **f6v is within the normal distribution of herbal A folios.**

## y-Initial Rate Distribution Among Herbal A Folios

The distribution is wide and appears bimodal:
- **High y-initial group** (>20%): ~30 folios, mean ≈ 25-30%
- **Zero y-initial group** (0%): ~12 folios (f49r, f8v, f32r, f32v, f35r, f42v, f45r, f47r, f47v, f13r, f20r, f22r, f30v, f4r, f7r)

Zero-y-initial folios include f49r (known structural anomaly: 0% y-initial confirmed in exp_055) and f8v, f32r, f32v, f35r, f42v, f45r, f47r, f47v. These span different quires and folio positions, suggesting the zero-y-initial property may reflect scribal hand or content category rather than folio position.

## Correlation: y-Rate vs Folio Line Count

Within herbal A folios, there is no clear correlation between folio length and y-initial rate (folios with 21 lines range from 4.8% to 38.1%). The y-initial rate appears to be a folio-specific property independent of text length.

## H065 Verdict: NOT SUPPORTED
f6v's 38.1% y-initial rate is NOT due to unusually long paragraphs. Every herbal A folio is a single paragraph; f6v is no different. f6v ranks #2 among herbal A folios by y-initial rate, but f53v (38.5%) is higher. The y-initial rate variation across herbal A folios (0% to 38.5%) is a real phenomenon but is NOT explained by paragraph structure differences — it likely reflects scribal hand variation or content-category differences among the herbal folios.

## Finding (F065)
All Currier A herbal folios are single-paragraph text blocks (75/75 folios show n_paras=1). y-initial rate variation among herbal A folios (0% to 38.5%) is not explained by paragraph length — it is a folio-level property that varies independently. The bimodal distribution (zero-y cluster vs 20-35% cluster) suggests two scribal populations or content sub-categories within the herbal section. f6v's 38.1% is within the normal range of the high-y group; f53v is higher (38.5%). The zero-y-initial folios (f49r, f8v, f32r, f32v, f35r, f42v, f45r, f47r, f47v and others) warrant investigation as a distinct scribal or content sub-group.
