**H_new_AT (B pharma ot-LF and l-LF routing):** ANSWERED with multiple confirmations.
- ot-LF: ot self-chains near line end (ot at pen, CONFIRMED). t elevated at pos1 (dir).
- l-LF: ok at pos2 (CONFIRMED); l at pen (CONFIRMED) — extends F423 l-chain to line-terminal region.
- ok-LF: a at pen (CONFIRMED).

**Pharma grammar pattern emerging:** B pharma LF classes show "onset-class persistence" in different positions:
- ch-LF: ch at pos1 (F449) — ch persists from LF back to line start
- ot-LF: ot at pen — ot persists from LF to penultimate
- l-LF: l at pen — l persists from LF to penultimate (also ok at pos2)
- ok-LF: a at pen — ok-lines close with a-suffix words before LF

A distinct grammar pattern: B pharma LF class determines what appears at PENULTIMATE (ch→ch, ot→ot, l→l) OR at LINE-START (ch→ch). The penultimate rule creates a doublet structure [X...X] at LF and pen for class X.

**A herbal_a d-LF and ch-LF routings (F453, F454):** These extend the A herbal_a section grammar characterization:
- d-LF uses ot at pos1 and sh at pen — consistent with A herbal_a using ot as a "bridge class" between d and sh domains

- Both findings extend F406 (A formula types) with LF-conditional routing rules
