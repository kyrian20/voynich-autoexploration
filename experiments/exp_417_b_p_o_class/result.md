## Interpretation

**p→o enrichment is a category-level preference, not a formula-word preference.** Both pos1 and LL o-class vocabularies are highly distributed (17 and 23 types for N=21 and N=25 respectively). The enrichment is not driven by a few formula words. The MID o-vocabulary is dominated by 'op-' compound words ('opchedy', 'opchdy', 'opchey' = o+p+ch compounds), which account for the largest single counts in p-formula MID positions.

**pos2 after p→o at pos1 is near-Markovian** (no E≥2.0 at N≥3). The slight elevation of sh(1.65×) and ch(1.72×) at pos2 is consistent with the overall B body structure after o-class words, not a p-formula-specific rule.

**The o-class preference in p-formula lines reflects a semantic/functional category:** p-formula lines prefer words from the broad 'o' onset class (which includes 'or', 'oraiin', and many 'op-' compounds). The 'op-' compound pattern in MID suggests that p-formula content includes many words beginning with the o+p onset combination — potentially a word-internal compound structure where o-onset words contain a p within their body. This is consistent with p being a gallows class (F387/F412): p appears both as LF (the p-formula LF) and within o-onset compound words in the MID of p-formula lines.
