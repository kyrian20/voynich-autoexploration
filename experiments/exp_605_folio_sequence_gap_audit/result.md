## Interpretation

A matching gap list verifies only that the transcription headers reflect known foliation gaps. It does not prove no other pages were lost, and text-only evidence cannot reliably infer missing leaves that disappeared before numbering. Any one-sided folio header should be checked against physical manuscript images and the transcription's editorial conventions before being classified as a missing page.
