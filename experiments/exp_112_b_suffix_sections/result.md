### What the Partition Actually Is: A Gradient, Not Categorical

The 3-way partition is NOT a categorical switch (herbal=edy-only, cosmo=eedy-only, stars=eey-only). Instead, it's a GRADIENT:
- **-edy dominance erodes**: herbal=70-87% → cosmo=43-76% → stars=23-61%
- **-eey increases**: herbal=0-30% → cosmo=6-21% → stars=25-51%
- **-eedy intermediate**: peaks at cosmo for qo-prefix stems, remains second-level in stars

The gradient is MONOTONIC across all tested onset classes for -eey (stars always highest). This is a consistent signal, not noise.

### The B-Suffix Section Structure

**Three-layer B-suffix architecture**:
1. **Universal B-baseline** (herbal-like): -edy dominant for ch/sh; balanced for qok/ok

## Combined Finding (F119)

**H220 CONFIRMED PARTIAL (F119)**: The secondary B-suffix is a SECTION-LEVEL REGISTER marker forming a monotonic gradient:
- **-eey gradient (UNIVERSAL)**: stars > cosmo > herbal for EVERY tested onset class — stars B is "eey-heavy"
- **-eedy elevation (stem-specific)**: cosmo B elevates -eedy for qo-prefix stems (qok=35%, qot=41%, ok=38% eedy) but not for ch/sh (which remain edy-dominant everywhere)

- **Stars-exclusive**: triple-vowel extended forms (oteeey=7, cheeey=6, lkeeey=7) found ONLY in stars B

**KEY FINDING (F119)**: Voynichese has a THREE-TIER dialect/section/register structure for B-paradigm suffixes:
1. **Tier 1 (Dialect)**: B-paradigm uses -edy/-eedy/-eey vs A-paradigm uses -ol/-or/-y (F114, F115)
2. **Tier 2 (Stem class)**: Within B-dialect, ch/sh are Simple-B (-edy dominant) vs qok/ok are Complex-B (balanced across B-suffixes) (F118)
3. **Tier 3 (Section)**: Within B-dialect, -eey increases from herbal→cosmo→**stars** (universal across all onset classes); -eedy peaks at cosmo for qo-prefix classes

The three-tier structure means B-dialect words carry simultaneous information about dialect, onset class, and section — a multi-dimensional morphological encoding system. The monotonic gradient (not categorical switch) suggests the sections represent a continuum of the same text tradition rather than completely distinct varieties.
