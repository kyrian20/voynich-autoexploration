## Combined Finding (F102)

**H172 STRONGLY CONFIRMED**: p+ch IS A's para-starter for ch-stems (26.9% PS, 61.5% LFirst). f+ch also para-starter (21.4%). t+ch = weak para (4.1%). k+ch = very weak (3.5%). y/d+ch = line-openers (0%). bare_ch = mid-line (0.3%). Complete 5-level ch-stem hierarchy: bare(0%) ≈ y/d(0%) << t/k+ch(3-4%) << f+ch(21%) < p+ch(27%).

**H171 SUPPORTED**: Platform gallows (p, f) are MORE para-start specialized than regular gallows (t, k). Consistent ordering: p > f > t > k for bare gallows PS% (A: 40.2%/24.2%/10.2%/5.7%) AND for gallows+ch PS% (26.9%/21.4%/4.1%/3.5%). Platform/regular distinction encodes PARA-START STRENGTH.

**H173 STRONGLY CONFIRMED**: sh-stem hierarchy identical to ch-stem: p+sh/t+sh = para-starters (33%/29% PS); y+sh/d+sh = line-openers (0%); bare_sh = mid-line (1%). Gallows-prefix para-start mechanism is UNIVERSAL across all A stem classes.

**H174 COMPLETE PREFIX MATRIX**: Three tiers clearly separated in A:
1. PARA-LEVEL: {p, f, t} prefix on any stem (PS=20-56%), plus bare_p/bare_f
2. LINE-OPENER: {y, d} prefix on any stem (PS=0%, LFirst=55-86%)
3. MID-LINE: bare_ch/bare_sh/qo+k/qo+t (PS=0-1%, LFirst=7-27%)

**REVISED MASTER MODEL (F102): The Voynichese Positional Grammar is a TWO-DIMENSION PREFIX SYSTEM:**
- DIMENSION 1 (Positional Level): Gallows-prefix (p/f/t) = PARA-LEVEL; y/d-prefix = LINE-LEVEL; qo-prefix or bare non-gallows = MID-LINE
- DIMENSION 2 (Para-Start Strength within gallows): Platform gallows (p, f) = STRONGEST para-starters; Regular gallows (t) = WEAKER para-starters; k = WEAKEST

**UNIFIED A GRAMMAR MODEL:**
```
PARAGRAPH-OPENER (PS=20-56%): bare_p, bare_f, p+ch, p+sh, f+ch, t+sh
  → Platform gallows (p, f) prefix/bare MOST para-specialized

LINE-OPENER (PS=0%, LFirst=55-86%): y+ch, d+ch, y+sh, d+sh
  → y and d prefix = within-para line-start markers

MID-LINE (PS≈0%, LFirst=7-27%): bare_ch, bare_sh, qo+k, qo+t
  → Bare non-gallows or qo-prefixed = medial position

LINE-CLOSER (LLast=15-20%): bare_ch, d+suffix words
  → d-onset bare forms (dal, dar, dam) terminate lines
```
