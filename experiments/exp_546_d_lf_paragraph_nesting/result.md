**NEW: A d→d CL CONF (E=1.77×/obs=101)** — d-LF lines are followed by another d-LF line at CONF level. D has strong paragraph-level self-succession: a line ending in d often precedes another d-terminal line. This is the dominant CL signal from d-LF, stronger than d→qot.

**d→qot CL: E=1.37×/obs=10** — borderline relative to CONF threshold (need E≥1.5×); F491 established CONF in original experiment (possibly with slightly different baseline or N). The current analysis is consistent with sub-CONF or just-below-CONF — not contradicting F491 but not confirming it here either.

### Conditional Analysis: does qot-opener enhance d→qot CL?

| Opener class | N CL pairs | d→d obs/N | d→d E | d→qot obs/N | d→qot E |
|-------------|------------|-----------|-------|-------------|---------|
| qot-opener | 20 | 9/20 | **2.45×** | 2/20 | 4.26× |
| ot-opener | 21 | 11/21 | **2.85×** | 0/21 | 0.00× |
| other opener | 270 | 81/270 | 1.62× | 8/270 | 1.34× |

**H_new_FI: NEGATIVE** — the qot-opener at pos1 does NOT enhance d→qot CL routing. qot-opener d-LF lines have qot on next line at obs=2/20 (E=4.26× but N=20 gives expected=0.47 — only 2 obs, far below reliability). ot-opener d-LF lines have qot ZERO on next line. The within-line structure [qot/ot(pos1)...d(LF)] (F556) and the CL d→qot coupling (F491) appear to be **independent structural tendencies** that do not form a nested grammatical unit.

**Side finding**: Template-opener d-LF lines (qot/ot at pos1) show enhanced d→d CL: qot-opener → d→d at E=2.45×/obs=9; ot-opener → d→d at E=2.85×/obs=11. Both above overall d→d (1.77×). Template-opened d-terminal lines tend to chain specifically to MORE d-terminal lines (obs too small for formal CONF/dir but directionally consistent).
