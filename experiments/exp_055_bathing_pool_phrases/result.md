## Results — H059: Bathing-Pool Folio Phrase Structures

### What Drives the Lag=13 Autocorrelation in f75v, f82v, f84v?

**The lag=13 repeating words are high-frequency bio-section vocabulary**, not a distinctive phrase template:
- f75v: 'qokain' (3×), 'ol' (2×)
- f82v: 'qokain' (3×), 'qokedy' (1×), 'qokeedy' (1×)
- f84v: 'shedy' (2×), 'okedy' (2×)

`qokain`, `ol`, `qokeedy`, `shedy`, `chedy`, `qokedy` are all high-frequency Bio section words. They repeat at lag=13 by statistical chance — not because of a 13-word template.

### Cross-Folio Bigram Sharing (Pool Folios vs Other Bio Pairs)

| Pair | Shared bigrams |
|------|---------------|
| f75v ∩ f82v | 6 |

**Pool folios share 2-8 bigrams with each other — similar to other bio pairs.** There is no evidence of a distinctive phrase template shared specifically by these three folios.

The 15 bigrams appearing in 2+/3 pool folios are all standard high-frequency bio bigrams: `qol chedy`, `ol chedy`, `ol shedy`, `qokain okal`, `qokain otain` — typical bio-section phrases found across the section, not pool-folio-specific.

### H059 Verdict: NOT SUPPORTED
The lag=13 signal in f75v, f82v, f84v is statistical coincidence driven by high-frequency bio words repeating at various lags. Cross-folio bigram sharing is 2-8 bigrams, indistinguishable from other bio folio pairs. These folios do not share a distinctive phrase structure not found in other bio folios.

## Results — H063: y-Initial Line-Opening by Section

### Section y-Initial Rates (First Word of Line)

| Section | Lines | y-initial% |
|---------|-------|-----------|
| Astro (A) | 56 | **23.2%** |
| Pharma (P) | 224 | 17.9% |
| Special (S) | 1164 | 16.6% |
| Herbal (H) | 1608 | **14.7%** |
| Text (T) | 226 | 14.6% |
| Bio (B) | 745 | **7.5%** |
| Cosmo (C) | 107 | 7.5% |

**Bio and cosmo have the LOWEST y-initial rates (7.5%)** — half the herbal rate. Astro has the highest (23.2%), though with only 56 lines.

Since y-initial words are line-CONTINUATION markers (exp_054/F061), sections with high y-initial rates have more text organized as long within-paragraph runs. Astro text (in concentric rings) may have many continuation lines relative to paragraph starts. Bio's low y-initial rate suggests bio has shorter paragraphs or more paragraph starts per total lines.

### Per-Folio Outliers in Currier A

Top y-initial folios (≥20 lines, Currier A):
- f6v: **38.1%** (8/21 lines) — strong outlier
- f17v: 26.1%, f3r: 25.0%, f93r: 21.9%
- f49r: 0.0% (0/21 lines) — complete absence

f6v appears to have an unusual text structure with extraordinarily high line-continuation rate. f49r (with its margin annotations and key-like features) has zero y-initial lines.

### H063 Verdict: PARTIALLY SUPPORTED
y-initial words vary substantially by section (7.5% bio to 23.2% astro) and by folio (0% to 38%). The variation is consistent with y-initial being a line-continuation marker: sections with long paragraphs (astro/pharma) have high y-initial rates; bio (shorter, formulaic paragraphs) has low. The section correlation reflects paragraph structure differences, not a section-specific vocabulary preference.

## Combined Finding (F062)
Lag=13 in bio pool folios is statistical artifact: cross-folio bigram sharing among f75v, f82v, f84v (2-8 bigrams) is indistinguishable from other bio pairs; the repeating words are standard high-frequency bio vocabulary. y-Initial line rates vary by section (astro 23.2% to bio 7.5%), consistent with y-initial being a continuation-line marker: sections with longer paragraphs show higher y-initial rates. Per-folio outliers: f6v (38.1%) has an extreme continuation structure; f49r (0%) has no y-initial lines. The per-section y-initial rate pattern inversely correlates with the exp_043 bio formulaicity finding: bio is the most phrase-repetitive section AND has the fewest line-continuation markers, suggesting bio text is organized in short, formulaic paragraphs rather than long flowing passages.
