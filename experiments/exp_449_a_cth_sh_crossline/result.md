**H_new_L ANSWERED (NEGATIVE): A cth→sh cross-line (E=1.93×/N=12) is NOT section-specific beyond A-dialect's general herbal dominance.**

1. **Herbal concentration matches baseline**: 9/12=75% herbal matches A-dialect's ~74% herbal proportion of cross-line pairs. No herbal enrichment beyond baseline. cth→sh is distributed proportionally to A-dialect's herbal concentration.

2. **Word pairs are class-level**: Both cth-words at LL and sh-words at LF are diverse (8+ distinct types in each position). No single word pair dominates. cth→sh is a class-level onset coupling, not word-specific.

3. **sho-prefix slightly depleted after cth-LL (58% vs 79.8% baseline)**: If anything, cth-LL precedes slightly more diverse sh-words than typical. No specific sh-subtype enrichment.

4. **A s→s cross-line (N=8) has 2 pairs from f67r2 (bio_stars)**: f67r2 is the section with super-geometric y-chains (F410) and elevated sequence structure. The s→s cross-line pairs from f67r2 may reflect the unusual structural density of that folio. N=2 is directional only.

**Summary**: The A cth→sh cross-line E=1.93×/N=12 is a genuine A-dialect class-level onset coupling (not section-specific), distributed proportionally to A's herbal-dominant corpus. It reflects A's general cth-class positional tendency (cth appears at LL; the next line opens with sh) without section specificity.
