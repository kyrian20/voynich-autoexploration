## Interpretation

**Two y-formula paths to ch at pos2 are section-differentiated:**

1. **y→sh→ch** (E=2.88×/N=8): distributed across herbal(3)+bio(1)+pharma(4). The classic content-formula type from F394/F398. Uses varied sh-onset words at pos1.

2. **y→o→ch** (E=2.58×/N=6): concentrated in bio(2)+pharma(4), absent from herbal. Uses 'or' at pos1 in 5/13 y→o lines (the word 'or' is the dominant o-class word at pos1 in y-LF lines).

**The y→o→ch formula type is a pharma+bio variant**: in pharma and bio sections, the y-LF grammar uses o-onset (specifically 'or') at pos1 and routes to ch at pos2. In herbal, y-LF uses sh at pos1 to route to ch (y→sh→ch). Both are "content-formula" paths (routing to ch at pos2), but via different pos1 classes and concentrated in different sections.

**'or' at pos1 in y→o lines**: 'or' is a short, simple o-class word (2 characters). Its dominance at pos1 in y→o lines suggests that when y-LF lines use o-class at pos1, they specifically favor the minimal form 'or'. This is consistent with y-LF grammar selecting specific formula words in specific section contexts.

**y→sh→qok depletion in y→sh lines**: qok=0.58×(N=4) — depleted at pos2 when y→sh. This is consistent with y-LF avoiding the template-class qok path after sh (the y-formula uses ch not qok as pos2).
