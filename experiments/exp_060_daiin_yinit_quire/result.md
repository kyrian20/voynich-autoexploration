# Experiment exp_060: daiin × y-Initial Correlation + Quire Clustering (H069, H072)
Date: 2026-04-09

## H069: daiin Rate vs y-Initial Rate Across Herbal A Folios

### Correlation Results

| Correlation | r |
|-------------|---|
| daiin rate vs y_initial_rate (n=72) | **-0.297** |
| daiin rate vs y_cont_rate (n=71) | **-0.297** |

A **moderate negative correlation** exists: higher daiin rate tends toward lower y-initial rate. Not strong, but consistent.

### Aiin-Dominant vs y-Dominant: daiin Rates

| Group | Folios | Mean daiin% |
|-------|--------|------------|
| Aiin-dominant (y@cont <5%) | 14 | **6.88%** |
| y-dominant (y@cont >20%) | 22 | **3.50%** |

Aiin-dominant herbal A folios have **~2× higher daiin** than y-dominant folios. The highest daiin folios (f32v=15.15%, f30v=11.86%, f22r=10.64%) are all aiin-dominant.

### Interpretation

In the aiin-dominant register:
- Lines rarely start with y-initial words (0-5% vs 16-40% in y-dominant)
- Lines frequently end with -aiin words (aiin-last 18-53%)
- daiin (specifically the word d+aiin) is highly represented (~2× more frequent)

This suggests **daiin and y-initial are competing line-continuation strategies**. In the aiin-dominant register, -aiin endings (including the word daiin) encode what y-initial encodes in the y-dominant register. The two registers may represent the same underlying grammatical function implemented differently.

**Quire F has 5/8 aiin-dominant herbal folios (62.5%)**. Quire D has 3/6 aiin-dominant (50%). Quires B, O, Q have zero aiin-dominant folios.

### Aiin-Dominant Folios by Quire

**Quire F (5 folios)**: f42r, f42v, f45r, f47r, f47v — a continuous run of herbal folios in quire F that are almost all aiin-dominant. Note: f44r (quire F) and f44v (quire F) are y-dominant exceptions within quire F.

**Quire D (3 folios)**: f30v, f32r, f32v — 3 of 6 herbal D folios are aiin-dominant; no y-dominant folios exist in quire D.

**Quires B, O, Q**: zero aiin-dominant folios — exclusively y-dominant.

### H072 Verdict: PARTIALLY SUPPORTED

Aiin-dominant folios cluster most strongly in Quires D (50%) and F (62%). Quires B, O, Q are exclusively y-dominant. This partial clustering is consistent with a scribal-hand hypothesis: quires D and F may have been written (or copied) by a different scribe who used the aiin-dominant grammar convention. However, the clustering is imperfect — aiin-dominant folios also appear in quires A, C, E, G, suggesting either multiple scribes or a content-driven effect within quires.

## Combined Finding (F067)

H069 PARTIALLY SUPPORTED: daiin rate is negatively correlated with y-initial rate across herbal A folios (r = -0.297). Aiin-dominant herbal folios have 2× higher daiin (6.88%) than y-dominant folios (3.50%), suggesting daiin and y-initial are competing line-continuation strategies — the aiin-dominant register uses -aiin endings (including daiin) where the y-dominant register uses y-initial words.

H072 PARTIALLY SUPPORTED: Aiin-dominant herbal A folios cluster significantly in Quires D (50% aiin-dominant) and F (62%), while Quires B, O, Q have zero aiin-dominant folios. This quire-level clustering supports a scribal-hand explanation for the two-register system, though the imperfect clustering suggests additional content or folio-position effects. The two-register system may reflect a historical process: different quires were compiled by different scribes with different grammatical conventions, but a single scribe within quire F still wrote some y-dominant folios (f44r, f44v), so the scribal-hand boundary is not perfectly correlated with quire.
