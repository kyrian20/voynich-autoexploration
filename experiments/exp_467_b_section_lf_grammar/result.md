## Interpretation

**H_new_AG CONFIRMED: B herbal/pharma/cosmo have section-specific LF markers.**

### B-dialect section LF grammar taxonomy (NEW)

**B astro:** {qok↑(1.78×), qot↑(1.94×), qo↑(1.60×), s↑(1.31×)}; {y↓(0.47×), t↓(0.70×), ok↓(0.68×), ot↓(0.59×)}
- F431 established qok alone. **qot and qo are also elevated** — B astro is a "q-bench triplet" section (all three q+bench onset classes: qok, qot, qo simultaneously elevated at LF)
- y is massively depleted in B astro (0.47×/N=45) — the opposite of B herbal

**B herbal:** {t↑(2.34×), y↑(1.52×), ot↑(1.55×), p↑(1.53×)}; {qok↓(0.54×), s↓(0.54×), ok↓(0.50×), ch↓(0.59×), o↓(0.51×)}
- t-LF elevation 2.34× is the strongest section LF marker in B after B astro qot↑(1.94×)
- The {t, y} combination matches within-line formula types: t→ch→y is not a formula type but t at LF → ch at pos2 (E=4.09×/N=7) directional — may indicate a B herbal formula starting with t

**B pharma:** {ok↑(1.47×), o↑(1.53×), ch↑(1.30×)}; {qok↓(0.52×), t↓(0.62×), qot↓(0.42×)}
- ok and o at LF elevated — "open gallows" classes. Not the l-LF expected (l is NOT elevated at pharma LF: E=0.82×); F423 l-chain concentration is a within-line and MID phenomenon, not a LF marker
- pharma qok-LF DEPLETED (0.52×) — opposite of astro qok-LF (1.78×)

**B cosmo:** {t↑(2.23×), p↑(2.60×,dir)}; {qot↓(0.00×), qo↓(0.28×), ch↓(0.38×)}
- t-LF in cosmo (2.23×) parallels t-LF in herbal (2.34×)
- qot-LF is COMPLETELY ABSENT from B cosmo (0 observations in 116 lines)
- This matches A cosmo qot-LF = 0.06× (1 case in 332 lines): **BOTH A and B cosmo show near-zero qot-LF**

**t-LF is elevated in the cosmo section (f85-102) in BOTH dialects.** Both A cosmo (1.63×/N=30) and B cosmo (2.23×/N=16) show t-LF elevation. This is a rare cross-dialectal LF grammar agreement — the cosmo content (f85-102) consistently uses t-onset words to open lines in both A and B dialect text.

**t-LF shows A/B inversion in the "astronomical" range:** A stars (f57-72) has t↑ (F446), while B astro (f73-84) has t↓. These are different folio ranges (different content), so this is a content-specific result rather than a direct A/B inversion.
