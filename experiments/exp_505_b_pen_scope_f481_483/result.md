## Interpretation

**H_new_CJ ANSWERED: F481 has cross-section signal; F482 and F483 are B astro-specific.**

**F481 (qok→sh@pen):** Broader than B astro — B pharma also shows dir (E=2.36×/N=7). Combined qok-LF → sh@pen is a B-dialect tendency, confirmed in B astro and directional in B pharma.

**F483 (l→ch@pen):** B astro-specific. B pharma l-LF uses l-self at pen (CONF, new finding), NOT ch. Different LF classes use different pen routing in different sections.

**Self-chaining at pen by section:**
- B pharma: l-self (CONF, new), ot-self (CONF, F460)
- B astro: d-self (CONF, F482), ot-self near threshold
- B herbal: ot-self (dir, N=3)

The self-chaining pattern at pen is section-specific: each section has its own LF class that self-chains at pen. This mirrors the pos1/pos2 section-specific routing patterns found earlier.
