# exp_228: H428 — qok→ch Markov Transition

**Date**: 2026-04-11  
**Hypothesis**: H428 — Does adding a soft Markov transition bias (when previous word is qok-onset, next word preferentially ch-onset with probability p_qokch) fix qok_ch (77.5% FAIL → PASS) without regressing the 12 currently passing metrics from exp_226?  
**Status**: H428 CONFIRMED — qok_ch FIXED (77.5%→93.1% PASS). **13/18 PASS — NEW PROGRAM BEST** (+1 from exp_226's 12/18). All 12 prior passes maintained.

---

## Design

Single change from exp_226: add a soft Markov transition to the copy-buffer word selection for MID positions.

**Mechanism**: track `prev_onset` (the onset of the last word placed). When `prev_onset == 'qok'` and `random() < p_qokch`, draw the next word from ch-onset words in the copy buffer (filtered from the WINDOW=20 deque). If no ch-onset words are available in the buffer, fall back to uniform draw. When `prev_onset != 'qok'` or the Markov draw fails, draw uniformly from the full buffer.

The Markov bias is applied only in the MID copy path (fraction cp=0.55 of MID words that come from the buffer). LF and LL words are generated by their respective rules (unchanged from exp_226).

All other parameters identical to exp_226: cp=0.55, mp=0.90, p_blind=0.30, p_lf=1.0, edy-preserving fresh-fallback, VMS MID onset for MID.

**Parameter sweep**: p_qokch ∈ {0.00, 0.02, 0.04, 0.06, 0.08, 0.12} at N=10 each. Best value selected as the p_qokch giving qok_ch closest to VMS 1.267 within the pass zone [1.014, 1.520]. Full run N=30 at p_qokch=0.12 (best from sweep).

## Interpretation

### Finding 1: qok_ch fixed by Markov transition (77.5%→93.1% PASS)

qok_ch increases from 0.982 (exp_226) to 1.180 (exp_228) — an increase of 0.198, moving from 77.5% FAIL to 93.1% PASS. The mechanism is direct: when the previous word is qok-onset, the next word is preferentially drawn from ch-onset words in the copy buffer. The onset-preserving modification then maintains the ch-onset in the output. The qok→ch enrichment is mechanistically produced.

**Sweep diagnostic**: the theoretical prediction (qok_ch ≈ 1 + p_qokch × (1/ch_rate - 1) ≈ 1 + p_qokch × 5.5) substantially overestimates the actual effect. The sweep shows an effective slope of approximately 1 + p_qokch × 1.5:
- At p_qokch=0.12: theoretical=1.66, actual=1.166-1.180.

Root causes of the discrepancy:
1. The Markov bias applies only to the cp=0.55 fraction of MID words that come from the buffer; the fresh-word fraction (0.45 of MID) and all LF/LL words are unbiased.
2. The buffer may not always contain ch-onset words → fallback triggered; with WINDOW=20 and ch_rate≈15%, the buffer contains ≈3 ch-onset words on average — present but not guaranteed.
3. The modified copy may re-enter the buffer, partially diluting the ch enrichment over multiple generations.

The practical implication: p_qokch must be higher than the theoretical prediction to achieve a given qok_ch target. p_qokch=0.12 achieves qok_ch=1.180 (93.1% of VMS 1.267), within the ±20% pass zone [1.014, 1.520].

### Finding 2: ss_mean improves toward VMS (1.326→1.276, still PASS)

ss_mean decreases from 1.326 (113.9% VMS) to 1.276 (109.6% VMS). The qok→ch bias explicitly redirects some qok-following words away from qok to ch, reducing qok→qok self-succession. Since ss_mean = (ss_qok + ss_ok + ss_ch)/3, a decrease in ss_qok lowers ss_mean. This moves ss_mean toward the VMS target (1.164). The decrease of 0.050 is mechanistically expected and beneficial — it brings ss_mean closer to the tolerance boundary without crossing the lower bound (0.931). ss_mean remains comfortably within the pass zone.

### Finding 3: All 12 prior passes maintained

Every metric that passed in exp_226 continues to pass in exp_228. Notably:
- edy_rate: 0.187 (unchanged — mechanism unrelated to bigram transitions)
- ss_y: improved 1.598→1.643 (further from the 80% threshold — positive side effect)
- y_lf: small decrease (3.520→3.419) — still PASS at 102.9%
- ch_ll: 1.062→0.992 — decreased toward VMS 0.939 (still PASS at 105.7%)

The ch_ll decrease is a secondary Markov effect: when qok→ch transitions are added, more ch-onset words appear in the MID position stream (not just at LL). This slightly raises global ch rate, making LL ch less enriched relative to global → ch_ll decreases. The effect (1.062→0.992, −0.070) is consistent with the added ch bias in MID.

### Finding 4: Remaining 5 failures unchanged in character

The 5 remaining failures are structurally identical to exp_226:
1. **adj_rate** (36.0% FAIL): unchanged (0.003 in both — onset+edy preserving mod structural)
2. **qok_ll** (126.9% FAIL): marginal improvement (0.760→0.755) — the qok→ch bias slightly reduces qok frequency in MID by diverting some qok-following positions to ch → qok_ll ratio decreases slightly. Still fails.
3. **am_lf_enr** (326.4% FAIL): slight increase (0.373→0.400) — within normal simulation variance; root cause (global am rate divergence) unchanged.
4. **am_mid_enr** (142.2% FAIL): slight improvement (0.286→0.277) — same root cause.
5. **word_len_mean** (122.7% FAIL): essentially unchanged (6.637→6.628) — the Markov bias doesn't affect word length.

None of the 5 failures are addressable by the Markov mechanism; each has an established architectural root cause.

## Limitations

1. **Target-awareness of p_qokch**: The parameter p_qokch=0.12 was selected by sweeping and choosing the value that brings qok_ch closest to VMS 1.267 within the pass zone. This is target-calibrated. The result (qok_ch=1.180, 93.1% PASS) is conditional on this calibration — a different p_qokch would give a different qok_ch. At p_qokch=0.00 (no Markov bias), qok_ch=0.986 (77.8% FAIL), which is the baseline.
2. **Undershoot**: p_qokch=0.12 gives qok_ch=1.180 (93.1% of VMS 1.267). The pass zone is [1.014, 1.520]. The value is in the pass zone but below the VMS target. A higher p_qokch (e.g., 0.20) would push qok_ch higher but might approach or exceed the 1.267 target. The sweep did not extend to values that would overshoot.
3. **N=30 for full run**: Standard.
4. **Practical sweep discrepancy**: Theoretical prediction (slope≈5.5) substantially exceeds observed slope (≈1.5). The underestimate is mechanistically explained but means that the sweep must be done empirically — analytical predictions of the required p_qokch are unreliable.

## Conclusion

H428 CONFIRMED. Adding a soft qok→ch Markov transition at p_qokch=0.12 fixes qok_ch (0.982→1.180, 77.5%→93.1% PASS) and maintains all 12 prior passes. **13/18 PASS — new program best** (+1 from exp_226).

The qok→ch enrichment in VMS is now reproduced mechanistically: qok-onset words are more likely to be followed by ch-onset words in both VMS and the simulation. The Markov transition parameter p_qokch=0.12 is target-calibrated.

Secondary benefit: ss_mean improves from 113.9%→109.6% (moves toward VMS 1.164 without crossing the boundary).

**5 remaining failures** (adj_rate, qok_ll, am_lf_enr, am_mid_enr, word_len_mean) are structurally unchanged from exp_226 and require separate architectural mechanisms.
