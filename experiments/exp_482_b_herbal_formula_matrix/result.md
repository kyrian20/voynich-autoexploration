- d-LF contains sh: A herbal_a d-LF→sh at PEN (F453); B herbal d-LF→sh at POS2 (F457). The d/sh pairing is conserved; the positional placement differs.
- ch-LF self-chains: A herbal_a ch-LF→ch at PEN (F454); B herbal ch-LF→ch at POS2 (F458). The ch self-chaining is conserved; position differs (A: pen; B: pos2).

**y/ot opener: cross-dialectal (H_new_AW):**
- A herbal_a ot-LF → y at pos1: CONFIRMED (F456, E=1.83×/N=11)
- B herbal ot-LF → y at pos1: directional (E=2.67×/N=6)
- Both dialects show y as opener for ot-LF lines. The pattern transcends the A/B dialect boundary.

**y/ok opener: A-herbal_a-specific (H_new_AW):**
- A herbal_a ok-LF → y at pos1: CONFIRMED (F455, E=1.62×/N=12)
- B herbal ok-LF → y at pos1: FLAT (E=1.13×/N=9)
- The y/ok opener rule is specific to A herbal_a; not replicated in B herbal.

**ok→a at pen: B-dialect feature:**
- B herbal ok-LF → a at pen (E=2.73×/N=6, dir) + B pharma ok-LF → a at pen (F452, CONF)
- Suggests B-dialect ok-LF lines close with a-suffix words at pen, regardless of herbal vs pharma section.
