## Interpretation

**H_new_T ANSWERED (NEGATIVE for Prediction 1):** shedy at LL does NOT route to qok cross-line. Instead, shedy at LL → d (E=1.54×), matching the overall sh→d CL coupling. The within-line shedy→qok rule (F439) does NOT extend cross-line. shedy words at LL "switch" their target from qok (within-line) to d (cross-line).

**Support for H_new_U (edy-body routing is context-dependent):** Across onset classes, edy-body words route to different targets within-line (self-succession in qok, qok in sh) vs cross-line:
- sh+edy: within=qok; cross-line=d
- qok+edy: within=qok self; cross-line=d (weak, 1.37×)
- ok+edy: within=l (F439); cross-line=y
None of the edy-body cross-line targets match the corresponding within-line target.

**NEW directional finding:** ch+edy at LL → qot CL (E=2.23×/N=7). The within-line ch/qot relationship (chedy → qot?) is not established. This cross-line coupling may reflect qot as a specialized "follow-ch" onset class cross-line.

**ok→y CL is NOT edy-body-specific:** Both ok+aiin (1.98×/N=13) and ok+edy (1.58×/N=7) drive the F438 ok→y CL rule. This differs from d+aiin→sh and qok+aiin→s (aiin-body specific). ok→y may be a genuine class-level cross-line rule without body-class specificity.

**General principle forming (H_new_U):** edy-body LL words do NOT continue their within-line self-succession cross-line; they route to different targets. aiin-body LL words are the reliable cross-line bridge predictors. The F441 principle (aiin-body = cross-line bridge) generalizes; edy-body = within-line only remains supported.
