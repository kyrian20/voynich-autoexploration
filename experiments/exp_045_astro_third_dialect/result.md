# Experiment exp_045: Astro/Cosmo Section as Third Morphological Dialect
Date: 2026-04-09

## Question (H056)
Is the astro/cosmo section (Hand 4, 9 folios, 471 tokens) morphologically intermediate between Currier A and B?

## Results

### Vocabulary Jaccard Distances

| Pair | Jaccard |
|------|---------|
| A ↔ B | 0.119 |
| A ↔ Astro | **0.046** |
| B ↔ Astro | **0.033** |

Astro has LOWER Jaccard with both A and B than A and B have with each other. This is partly a sample-size effect (471 tokens), but also reflects a genuinely distinct vocabulary. Astro is SLIGHTLY closer to A (Jaccard=0.046) than to B (Jaccard=0.033).

Astro vocabulary composition: 129 unique words, 24 A-only, 40 B-only, 161 in both A and B. The 40 B-only vs 24 A-only suggests marginal alignment with B at the lexical level.

### Suffix Distribution

| Suffix | A% | B% | Astro% | Astro position |
|--------|-----|-----|--------|---------------|
| -edy | 0.2% | 18.5% | **0.7%** | Intermediate (near A) |
| -ol | 19.6% | 7.2% | **13.8%** | Intermediate (between A and B) |
| -eedy | 0.1% | 7.7% | **0.7%** | Intermediate (near A) |
| -or | 13.9% | 3.9% | **6.7%** | Intermediate (between A and B) |
| -chy | 5.9% | 1.8% | **4.6%** | Intermediate (between A and B) |
| -ey | 7.8% | 8.5% | **15.2%** | **Above B** (anomalously high) |
| -eey | 5.6% | 8.5% | **11.3%** | **Above B** (anomalously high) |
| -y | 18.5% | 12.4% | **19.5%** | At A level |
| -ain | 3.1% | 8.6% | **2.8%** | Below A (less than A) |

5 of 11 suffixes are intermediate between A and B. 3 are above B (anomalously high). 2 are at A level or below.

### qo-Rate
- Currier A: 0.110
- Currier B (excluding Hand 4): 0.190
- Astro/Cosmo (Hand 4): **0.059** — BELOW both A and B

**Astro qo-rate is even lower than Currier A**, making it NOT intermediate on this measure. This is the strongest signal: Hand 4's astro/cosmo section avoids qo-prefix more than any other group.

### Interpretation

Astro/Cosmo (Hand 4) appears to be a **distinct morphological register** rather than a clean intermediate between A and B:
- **Resembles A** in: near-absent -edy, present -ol/-or/-chy (A-type suffixes), near-absent qo (even below A), -y high
- **Differs from both** in: -ey and -eey anomalously high (15.2% and 11.3%, above B); qo below A
- **Small sample**: 471 tokens limits statistical power

The anomalously high -ey/-eey may reflect circular ring text structure (the astro/cosmo folios feature text arranged in concentric rings, where word selection may be constrained by spatial fit). The near-zero qo and near-zero -edy make Hand 4 the most morphologically "pure A-type" of the Currier B-designated hands.

## Finding (F052)
Astro/Cosmo section (Hand 4, 9 folios, 471 tokens) has a morphological profile closer to Currier A than to Currier B on key markers: qo-rate=0.059 (below even A=0.110), -edy=0.7% (near A=0.2%, far below B=18.5%), -ol=13.8% (intermediate: A=19.6%, B=7.2%). However, -ey (15.2%) and -eey (11.3%) are anomalously high — above B levels — possibly a circular ring text effect. Vocabulary Jaccard: A↔Astro=0.046 > B↔Astro=0.033, confirming astro vocabulary is slightly closer to A than to B. The astro/cosmo section appears to be a morphologically distinct register (neither pure A nor pure B) with A-adjacency in its core suffix markers.

## H056 Verdict: PARTIALLY SUPPORTED
The astro/cosmo section is A-adjacent (not intermediate, not B-like) on core morphological markers (qo, -edy, -ol), but anomalous on -ey/-eey. Sample size (471 tokens) limits conclusions. The Currier "B" designation for Hand 4 may reflect character-set similarity rather than morphological similarity; on suffix paradigm metrics, Hand 4 aligns more with Currier A.
