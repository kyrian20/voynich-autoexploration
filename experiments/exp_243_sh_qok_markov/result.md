## Slope Analysis

From the sweep: E(sh→qok) = 0.962 + 2.25 × p_sh_qok (approximately linear).

To reach E=1.905: p_sh_qok ≈ (1.905 - 0.962) / 2.25 ≈ **0.42**.

This is far outside the viable range. At p_sh_qok=0.42, the mechanism would be drawing qok words from the buffer ~20% of the time after any sh-onset word, with severe effects on onset composition and buffer dynamics.

**The slope is ~1.5× that of qok→ch (exp_228 reported slope≈1.5 per unit p_qokch)**, but the target enrichment is also much higher relative to baseline (qok→ch baseline E≈0.97; sh→qok baseline E≈0.96). The qok→ch mechanism achieved its target (E≈1.27 vs VMS 1.27) with p_qokch=0.12 because the target enrichment was smaller (1.3× vs baseline).

## Interpretation

### The sh→qok enrichment is architecturally beyond the copy-buffer Markov approach

The copy-buffer Markov mechanism works when:
1. The target onset-Y is moderately enriched in the buffer relative to global rates
2. The required probability p_XY is small enough that its effect on other metrics is negligible
3. The target enrichment is moderate (~1.2-1.4×)

For sh→qok (E_target=1.967), the required p_sh_qok ≈ 0.42, violating condition 2. The ss_d regression at p_sh_qok=0.05 confirms that even small values disrupt self-succession dynamics.

The mechanism failure reveals that **the sh→qok enrichment in VMS is not generated by the same mechanism as qok→ch**. The qok→ch rule is a binary bias (after any qok word, the next word tends to be ch-onset); sh→qok may be a different kind of structural constraint.

### Candidate explanations for sh→qok enrichment

1. **Lexical co-clustering**: Specific sh-onset words (shedy, shol, etc.) are followed by specific qok-onset words (qokeedy, qokedy) as fixed or near-fixed phrases. This would be captured by a vocabulary-level rule, not an onset-level Markov rule.
2. **Section/folio structure**: sh-onset words are concentrated in certain folios/sections that also have high qok density — local concentration rather than sequential grammar.
3. **Grammatical slot**: sh-onset words fill a specific grammatical role (e.g., MID adjective) that is systematically followed by qok-onset words in a subject-head or modifier-head construction.

### Status of H446

H446 (sh→qok Markov rule via p_sh_qok): **NEGATIVE** — mechanism architecturally insufficient. The approach cannot approach the VMS target without causing regressions. The sh→qok enrichment is real (exp_242 confirmed, N=339) but requires a different mechanism explanation.

### Status of 19-metric battery

The baseline (p_sh_qok=0.00) is confirmed at 19/19. No regression in the battery from confirming that p_sh_qok=0 is the correct setting. The 19/19 result stands.

## Limitations

1. The ss_d regression at p_sh_qok=0.05 is borderline (120.6%, just above the 120% limit). This could be noise — at N=30 it is consistent, but might fluctuate at the boundary with different seeds.
2. The slope estimate (2.25 E-units per unit p_sh_qok) is linear interpolation from a 5-point sweep; the true relationship may be nonlinear.
3. The architectural ceiling (p_sh_qok→1.0) was not tested; the actual maximum achievable E was extrapolated from the slope.
