## Summary of New Findings (F152)

1. **daiin is the UNIQUE cth-determiner in A-dialect (3.19×)**: No other tested particle (aiin, ar, ol, chol, dain, qokeey, saiin) precedes cth-words at any non-zero rate. The enrichment is categorical, not gradual. daiin→cth is a syntactic/grammatical marker specific to cth-nominal phrases.

2. **daiin→cth = 0.0% in B-dialect**: Consistent with B's near-total cth depletion. The determiner function of daiin does not span dialects via cth.

3. **In B-dialect, daiin introduces sh/ch_e at 2.33×/2.11×**: While all other B-particles are DEPLETED before sh and ch_e (0.31×–0.45×), daiin is uniquely enriched. This is the functional mirror of daiin's A-dialect role: in each dialect, daiin exclusively introduces the "marked" onset class (cth in A; sh/ch_e in B).

4. **Structural parallel confirmed**: The determiner-like function of daiin is dialect-specific but structurally parallel:
   - A-dialect: daiin → [cth-nominal] (3.19× enrichment; 0× for all other particles)
   - B-dialect: daiin → [sh/ch_e-nominal] (2.33×/2.11× enrichment; 0.31×–0.45× for other particles)
