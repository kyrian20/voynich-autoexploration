# exp_415: A cth at pos1 Characterization

**Method:** For A cont lines with cth at pos1 (N=75): (1) compute LF onset distribution with enrichment vs A baseline; (2) compute pos2 distribution vs A baseline; (3) compute E_cond for each (LF, cth, pos2) combination; (4) compare to B (N=2).

## Interpretation

**A cth at pos1 is NOT a formula class.** Despite its high prevalence at pos1 in A (5.2% vs B's 0.09%), cth at pos1 shows:
1. Broadly distributed LF routing (no single dominant LF class at E>2×)
2. Near-Markovian pos2 routing (highest E_cond=1.81×, directional only)
3. No formula types — cth does not "route" pos2 in a formula-typed way

This contrasts with cth at pos2 in A, which IS formula-typed (F406: o→sh→cth=4.33×, qot→ch→cth=2.60×). In A, cth is a **formula DESTINATION** (strongly enriched as pos2 after specific LF→pos1 pairs) but a **non-formula SOURCE** (as pos1, it does not route pos2 in a formula way).

**What explains A cth at pos1?** cth is an A-dominant class (F392: A cth=0.88×/B cth=0.13× as LF frequency; F406: A pos1=cth=5.2% vs B≈0.1%). A's broader distribution of cth across positions (LF, pos1, pos2, MID) reflects A's general inclusion of cth as a body-class content word, not a structural marker. In B, cth is almost entirely absent from line-level grammar (near-zero at LF, pos1, MID) but appears in specific formula positions.

**A cth at pos1: moderate routing enrichment:**
- qo=2.32×(N=5) and ot=1.90×(N=4) are slightly elevated at pos2 after cth-pos1. These follow A's general preference for o-class and ot-class in body positions.
- d is the top LF class routing to cth-pos1 (d=1.61×, N=21/75), consistent with d being the dominant A LF class that distributes broadly across pos1 classes.
- qot and d together account for 36% of A cth-pos1 lines (27/75), both at E≈1.6×.

**Comparison to B cth distribution:** In B, cth appears at MID=1.16× (F392) but is near-zero at LF(0.13×), pos1(≈0%), and LL. B's cth grammar is MID-restricted. A's cth grammar is broadly distributed (LF/pos1/pos2/MID all substantial).

## Limitations

- All E_cond values below 2.0 — no formula types identified. This IS the finding (absence of formula structure at cth-pos1).
- LF enrichments are modest (E≈1.6× at best). No strong LF-class dependency.
- The qo=2.32×(N=5) and ot=1.90×(N=4) at pos2 are moderate and may reflect the general A o-class body preference rather than cth-specific routing.

## Conclusion

H630 ANSWERED. A cth at pos1 (N=75, 5.2%) is **not a formula class**: LF routing to cth at pos1 is broadly distributed (d and qot slightly enriched at 1.61-1.65×), and pos2 after cth is near-Markovian (highest E_cond=1.81×, directional). The high A cth-pos1 prevalence reflects A's general broad distribution of cth across positions (cth is a body-class content word in A), not a specific grammar rule. In contrast, cth in A IS formula-typed AS A DESTINATION at pos2 (F406: o→sh→cth=4.33×, qot→ch→cth=2.60×). This asymmetry — cth as formula destination but not formula source — is a distinctive A-grammar property.
