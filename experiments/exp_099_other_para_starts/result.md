**The key finding: bare k and t gallows are strongly enriched within the "OTHER" para-start category.**
- A: k-onset = 9.12× enrichment (15/62 = 24.2% of OTHER PS words)
- B: k-onset = 11.92× enrichment (8/39 = 20.5% of OTHER PS words)
- A: t-onset = 3.25× enrichment (18/62 = 29.0% of OTHER PS words)
- In contrast, o-onset, d-onset, and s-onset are all SUPPRESSED or neutral within the OTHER category

**The classifier coverage gap is solved**: The "OTHER" words at para-starts are not mystery words — they are **bare_k and bare_t gallows words** (e.g., kooiin, tol, koaiin, kydainy, tydlo, tol) that our classifier didn't categorize into PARA tier because PARA tier was defined only as {p,f}+stem. Bare gallows (k, t) ARE para-starters (established in F101: k=4.32× A, 9.91× B; t=2.72× A) but were missing from the positional tier classifier.

### Revised Positional Tier Classifier

The PARA tier must be **expanded** to include:
- bare_k (A=4.32× PS enrichment, B=9.91×)
- bare_t (A=2.72×, B=2.05×)

Current PARA tier (p+stem, f+stem) captures only 33-39% of para-starts. Adding bare_k and bare_t would capture an additional ~27-33% of para-starts. The extended PARA tier = {p,f}+stem + bare_k + bare_t.

### H184 Verdict: CONFIRMED — "OTHER" Para-Start Words Are Predominantly Bare k/t Gallows Words (9-12× Enriched Within OTHER); The Classifier Coverage Gap Is Explained by Missing bare_k and bare_t From PARA Tier; o-Onset, d-Onset, s-Onset Are SUPPRESSED at Para-Starts Even Within the OTHER Category; The PARA Tier Must Be Extended to Include bare_k and bare_t to Cover ~65-70% of All Para-Starts

## Combined Finding (F106)

**H184 CONFIRMED**: The "OTHER" para-start words are predominantly bare_k (A=9.12×, B=11.92×) and bare_t (A=3.25×, B=1.77×) gallows words. The classifier coverage gap (38-54% OTHER) is explained by the absence of bare_k and bare_t from the PARA tier. o-onset, d-onset, and s-onset are SUPPRESSED at para-starts even within the OTHER category. The correct extended PARA tier = {p,f}+any_stem + bare_k + bare_t. This should capture ~65-70% of all para-starts.

**H185 PARTIALLY CONFIRMED**: o-onset is mildly enriched at para-final line-last positions (A=1.52×, B=1.25×). B has stronger para-final specialization: sh-onset (2.88×), specific words okeey (16.4×), orol (21.9×), shedy (6.25×). A para-final is dominated by daiin (weak 1.27×). P-onset (PARA tier) is perfectly excluded from para-final positions (0.00× both dialects) — perfect complementary distribution between para-start and para-end.

**KEY FINDING (F106)**: The complete PARA tier should be {p/f}+stem + bare_k + bare_t. The dominant 2-char onset patterns at para-starts are: po-(22×), py-(19×) in A and ko-(38×!), pd-(23×) in B. These are the most discriminative single morphological signatures of paragraph openings in each dialect. The "po-" pattern in A (bare_p words beginning with po-) and "ko-" in B (bare_k-onset) are the strongest para-start indicators found so far.
