## Parsing Note

The script classified folios by range without dialect check for n≤102, creating an "A_herbal_b" (n=73-84) section of 700 lines. However, prior experiments establish that n=73-84 B-dialect folios form B_astro (~700 lines). The "A_herbal_b" component is therefore **predominantly B-dialect content**, not a clean A-dialect herbal extension. The combined A herbal (a+b) analysis is unreliable for establishing new A-dialect findings because "A_herbal_b" is mostly B_astro.

**Valid data: A herbal_a alone (n≤56, 1188 lines) — this is the same section as exp_506.**

## H_new_CM ANSWERED: Cannot confirm ot-LF pen rules within clean A herbal data

**ot → cth@pen: directional (E=3.58×/N=9, A herbal_a only)** — remains directional. cth is absent in A herbal_b pen. Adding more A sections (A cosmo, A stars) would use different semantic sections where cth@pen signal may be different. This rule may be A-herbal_a-SPECIFIC.

**ot-self@pen: directional (E=3.51×/N=8, A herbal_a only)** — remains directional. Cannot confirm within clean A sections with available data.

To confirm these rules, one would need either: (1) more A herbal_a ot-LF lines (the section has only 51 ot-LF lines total, 48 eligible — the entire section is already used), or (2) combine cleanly-identified A-dialect herbal folios from a different source split.

## Conclusions

**H_new_CM ANSWERED:** ot-LF → cth@pen and ot-self@pen remain **directional** within clean A herbal_a data. No additional clean A-dialect herbal data is available to push these over threshold. **F485 (ok-self@pen) is confirmed from A herbal_a alone** — independent of the parsing question for herbal_b.
