## 5. Summary: The pos2 Self-Chaining Rule

**Proposed generalization**: In A herbal_a, the onset class of the line-final word (LF) also appears at pos2 (the second word of the line) at elevated rate. This is confirmed for d, ch, cth, ok, sh, o and directional for ot, k, y, qot, s, qok.

**Exceptions/complications**:
- y-LF: y-self at pos2 (dir, E=7.87×) — consistent with self-chaining rule; F448's ok at pos2 not reproduced
- The rule applies to self-chaining (same onset at pos2 as at LF); F448's cross-class routing (y-LF → ok at pos2) would be a genuine exception

**Mechanistic interpretation**: The second word of an A herbal_a line systematically "echoes" the word class of the last word. This could reflect:
(a) A positional grammar rule: the second word slot marks the line's dominant onset class
(b) A formula type structure: lines are identified by onset class appearing at both pos2 and LF positions
