## Interpretation

**H_new_AP ANSWERED (negative at confirmation threshold):**

**A herbal_a does not have a clean LF→LL routing matrix at confirmed levels.** Despite N=838 total lines and N=44-137 per major LF class, no LF→LL routing rule reaches E≥1.5×/N≥10 confirmation.

**Directional findings worth noting:**
1. **y-LF → o at LL (E=2.10×/N=9, dir)**: y-LF lines end with o-class words more than expected. Connects to B herbal y-LF → ot at LL (exp_472, E=2.83×/N=6,dir) — different specific LL class (o vs ot) but similar pattern (y-LF lines prefer o-related classes at LL in both dialects).
2. **sh-LF → sh at LL (E=2.13×/N=6, dir)**: sh self-bracket at LL. Connects to F413 (A d-sh-bracket=3.78×/N=4 where d is LF and sh is BOTH pos1 AND LL). The sh→sh at LL here is a weaker version — sh as LF predicts sh at LL but below the bracket level.
3. **s-LF → ok at LL (E=2.42×/N=5, dir)**: new — s-opening lines tend to end in ok-class. This is the reverse of B's s→qok CL (F444: s-LL → qok-LF in B astro): in A herbal_a, s opens lines that close with ok (not qok).
4. **ot-LF → qok at LL (E=3.17×/N=4, dir)**: ot-opening lines close with qok. Very directional.

**Key negative result:** The d-LF→ch(1.39×/N=35) mild elevation — despite large N=35 — falls below the 1.5× threshold for confirmed routing. This means A herbal_a d-LF does NOT have a systematic LL routing rule. Compare B astro qok-LF where the within-line formula types (F426) determine routing: qok→sh→k, qok→ch→qo, qok→ch→ol. A herbal_a d-LF has diverse LL routing (d=32.8%, ch=25.5%, cth=5.8%, ok=2.9%) with only mild elevation at ch.

**Interpretation**: A herbal_a's LL grammar is governed primarily by:
1. The LL baseline distribution (d=28.3% and ch=18.4% dominate)
2. Body-conditional rules (F441: specific body words drive LL onset selection)
3. Bracket structures (F413: specific LF classes have pos1=LL coupling)
...rather than by a systematic LF→LL onset routing matrix. The VMS A-dialect encodes LL selection at the word level, not the LF-class level.

**Contrast with B**: B has confirmed formula types (F426: 10 formula types at E≥2×/N≥5) where LF class determines pos1 and pos2 routing at confirmed levels. A herbal_a has weaker LF→LL routing — consistent with A being "more Markovian" (92.4% near-Markovian vs B 90.7%, working_position §13.2).
