## Interpretation

**H_new_AQ ANSWERED (all directional):**

**No confirmed body-conditional bifurcation for ok in pharma** — N per body class (6-7) is too small for confirmation. The l at pos1 elevation in ok-LF lines (exp_473: E=2.37×/N=8) is distributed across both edy-body and other-body ok words; aiin-body shows no l elevation.

**NEW (directional): B pharma qok+edy → ch at pos1 (E=2.64×/N=6)** — this is a pharma-specific DEVIATION from F451's all-B rule (qok+edy → qok-self, E=1.52×). In B pharma, edy-body qok words drive ch at pos1 (not qok-self). This suggests the qok body-conditional rule is section-modified in pharma: the general edy→self-succession rule is partially overridden by the pharma formula grammar (which routes through ch more extensively, consistent with pharma y→o→ch formula type, F430).

**l at pos1 generalization**: Both ok and qok in B pharma show l elevated at pos1 for non-aiin body classes (ok+other: 2.72×/N=5; qok+other: 2.97×/N=5). This is the pharma l-grammar: l appears at pos1 in template-class LF lines regardless of which template class (ok vs qok). F423's pharma l-concentration extends beyond l-LF lines — l appears as the first continuation word in ok and qok LF lines too.
