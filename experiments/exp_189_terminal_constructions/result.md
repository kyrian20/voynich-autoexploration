## 2. H323: [ychor][cthy@second] — cth ENRICHED (4.1×) AT SECOND POSITION; BOTH CASES IN HERBAL, NOT SAME FOLIO; THIRD WORD IS ch-ONSET IN BOTH CASES

### Results (A-dialect ychor lines, n=13)

Second-position onset enrichment after ychor:
- cth: 2/13 = 15.4%, base 3.8% → **4.1×** (the only strongly enriched onset)
- ol: 1/13 = 7.7%, base 2.4% → 3.2× (single case)
- ch: 3/13 = 23.1%, base 19.8% → 1.2×

The 2 [ychor][cthy@second] lines:
| Folio | Full line |
|-------|-----------|
| f17.15 | `ychor cthy che'eky cheo otor oteol` |
| f45.9 | `ychor cthy chol qokom sy<->sa,ykchom` |

**Pattern**: In BOTH [ychor][cthy] cases, the THIRD word is ch-onset:
- f17.15: cthy → **che'eky** (ch_e onset)
- f45.9: cthy → **chol** (ch onset)

This suggests a potential 3-gram construction: **[ychor][cthy][ch-word]** — ychor introduces cthy at second position, which in turn introduces a ch-word at third position. This would be a 3-word sequence: y-opener → cth-second → ch-third, progressively moving toward medial onset classes.

**Folio distribution**: f17 and f45 are both in the herbal A section but 28 folios apart — NOT clustered in the same folio or quire. The construction is spread through herbal A.

### Assessment: H323 SUPPORTED

cth-onset is enriched 4.1× at second position after ychor (n=2; small but consistent with F167). The new finding is that BOTH [ychor][cthy] cases are followed by a ch-word at third position, suggesting [ychor][cthy][ch-word] is a 3-gram construction. Folios f17 and f45 (not same folio, both herbal A) — no folio clustering. The construction appears to be a structural feature of A-herbal, not a formulaic repetition.
