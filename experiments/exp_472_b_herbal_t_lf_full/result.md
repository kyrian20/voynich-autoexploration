## Interpretation

**H_new_AM ANSWERED (all directional):**

**B herbal t-LF routing** (directional formula structure):
- pos1: qok(2.28×/N=6) — template class opens after t-LF
- pos2: o(2.69×/N=5) — o-class appears at pos2
- LL: o/ol cluster(~2.0×/N=5,4) — o-class terminates the line
- Tentative structure: t[LF] → qok[pos1] → o[pos2] → ... → o/ol[LL]

**B cosmo t-LF vs B herbal t-LF**: B cosmo has qok elevated at pos2 (not pos1) and all-directional results. The qok appearance at different positions may indicate: B herbal t-LF has qok directly adjacent to the t-word (qok at pos1); B cosmo t-LF has qok offset by one word. However, N=16 for cosmo makes this comparison marginal.

**A cosmo vs B cosmo t-LF**: Cross-dialectal cosmo agreement is in LF-class elevation (both A and B cosmo have t-LF elevated), but within-line routing DIFFERS:
- B cosmo t-LF: ok/qok class at pos1 (template class)
- A cosmo t-LF: ot/ol class at pos1 (o+gallows class)

This is consistent with the general A/B grammar difference: B uses qok-class in template (pos1) roles; A uses ot/ol-class in analogous positions (parallel to F406/F408 pos1 distribution inversions).

**B herbal y-LF → ot at LL (E=2.83×/N=6, confirmed directional)**: This is the first confirmed-directional routing finding for y-LF in B herbal. The y-terminal lines consistently end with ot-onset words. Combined with y-LF → y at pos1 (y self-succession): B herbal y-LF lines open with y, have y at pos1 (self-succession), and close with ot at LL. The y[LF] → y[pos1] → ... → ot[LL] pattern is B herbal-specific.
