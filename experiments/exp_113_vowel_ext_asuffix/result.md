### Vowel-Length Gradient Analysis

**The longer the vowel string, the more stars-concentrated:**

| Suffix | n | herbal% | cosmo% | stars% |
|--------|---|---------|--------|--------|
| **-edy** | 2,822 | 12% | 45% | 36% |
| **-eedy** | 1,174 | 6% | 40% | 48% |
| **-eeedy** | 52 | 4% | 25% | **63%** |

| Suffix | n | herbal% | cosmo% | stars% |
|--------|---|---------|--------|--------|
| **-eey** | 1,728 | 14% | 18% | 47% |
| **-eeey** | 165 | 13% | 7% | **59%** |

**Stars concentration is MONOTONICALLY INCREASING with vowel-string length**:
- edy: stars=36% → eedy: stars=48% → eeedy: stars=63% (monotonic increase)
- eey: stars=47% → eeey: stars=59% (monotonic increase)

Cosmo shows the OPPOSITE pattern for the -ey chain:
- edy: cosmo=45% → eedy: cosmo=40% → eeedy: cosmo=25% (monotonic DECREASE with length)

This means cosmo and stars have DIFFERENT specializations within the B-paradigm suffix-length space:
- **Cosmo**: moderate-length vowel strings (-eedy, -eey) but NOT triple-vowel
- **Stars**: all vowel-string lengths, with EXTREME preference for longest forms (eeedy, eeey)

### Interpretation: A Vowel-Length Phonological Dimension in Stars B

The vowel-length gradient in stars B suggests a PHONOLOGICAL dimension that runs in parallel with the B-suffix selection:
- B-baseline (herbal): -edy and -eey in roughly balanced doses
- B-intermediate (cosmo): -eedy extension preferred, moderate vowel length
- B-extreme (stars): triple-vowel extensions (eeedy, eeey), maximum vowel length

This vowel-length dimension may represent a phonological feature (vowel length, tone, or stress pattern encoded as vowel repetition) that is section-specific in stars B. The fact that ok/ol/qo are eee-enriched while ch/sh are suppressed suggests the phonological feature applies preferentially to specific onset classes.

## Combined Finding (F120)

**H222 CONFIRMED (F120)**: Triple-vowel (eee) words are stars-dominant (17.25‰) and cosmo-suppressed (4.40‰). The vowel-length gradient is MONOTONICALLY stars-concentrated: edy→eedy→eeedy shows stars increasing from 36% to 63%; eey→eeey shows stars from 47% to 59%. Cosmo shows the OPPOSITE: edy→eedy→eeedy = 45%→40%→25% cosmo (decreasing with length). eee words avoid ch/sh (0.37-0.46×) and concentrate on ok/ol/qo (1.7-1.9×) — same Complex-B onset classes that use flexible B-suffixes. Stars-only eee forms: oteeey(7), lkeeey(7), teeedy(4), lkeeedy(4).

**H223 CONFIRMED PARTIAL (F120)**: A-dialect has a mild but genuine 2-level section variation: herbal-A/-y dominant (ch=29%, qok=37%, ot=31%); pharma-A/-ol dominant (ch=28%, qok=41%, ot=41%). Consistent across ALL tested onset classes. Pharma-A elevates -dy/-ey. Average diff = 8.9% (vs B's 31%). Entropy unchanged (~2.5-2.8 bits). The A-section variation is 3.5× weaker than B's 3-tier system.

**KEY FINDING (F120)**: The Voynichese suffix system has a FOUR-LEVEL hierarchical structure:
1. **Level 1 (Dialect)**: A vs B paradigm (F114)
2. **Level 2 (Stem class)**: ch/sh Simple-B (-edy); qok/ok Complex-B (flexible) in B; -y vs -ol in A by section (F118)
3. **Level 3 (Section)**: B: stars→-eey (universal); cosmo→-eedy (qo-prefix); A: herbal→-y; pharma→-ol (F119-F120)
4. **Level 4 (Vowel length)**: Stars-specific triple-vowel extension (eeedy, eeey) — only for Complex-B onset classes (F120)

The four-level hierarchy is PROGRESSIVELY FINER:
- Level 1 separates ~half the manuscript
- Level 2 distinguishes onset classes within dialect
- Level 3 separates sections within dialect (B: 3 sections; A: 2 sections)
- Level 4 separates a stars-specific phonological extension from other B-sections

This degree of hierarchical structure in the suffix system is strong evidence for a natural language morphological system rather than a simple substitution cipher — a cipher would not produce this multi-level grammatical regularity.
