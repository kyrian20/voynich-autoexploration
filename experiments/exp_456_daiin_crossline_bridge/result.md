## Interpretation

**H_new_S ANSWERED: d+a-body cross-line effect is CLASS-LEVEL (distributed), but daiin has a specific cross-line role (→ sh).**

1. **Class-level (distributed)**: The F441 d+a-body→sh enrichment comes from multiple d+a-body words (daiin/dar/dal/dam all contribute to sh). No single word monopolizes the coupling.

2. **daiin is a "pure sh bridge" in B**: daiin specifically at LL → sh elevated (E=2.42×), but s DEPLETED (0.39×) and qok near-baseline (0.87×). dar/dal/dain route to both sh AND qok AND ch. daiin's cross-line profile is narrower than the class average.

3. **A-dialect daiin is sh+s bridge**: In A, daiin at LL predicts both sh (E=1.54×, N=63) and s (E=1.75×). This A/B difference is consistent with A-dialect's broader s-LF usage and A daiin's position-neutral distribution (F433).

4. **F441 is confirmed CLASS-LEVEL**: The d+a-body → sh effect (E=1.99× overall) is not driven by daiin alone but distributed across daiin/dar/dal/dam/dain. The class body (any d-word with 'a' as first body char) is the correct predictor level.
