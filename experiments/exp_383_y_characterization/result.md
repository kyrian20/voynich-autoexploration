### Finding 1: y Is a LF-FORMULA CLASS — the Third (A=2.28×, B=3.52×); LF-Class Ranking B: s(4.73×) > y(3.52×) > d(2.29×)

A y: LF=2.28×, pos1=0.51×, MID=0.88×, LL=0.61×. All three share: LF-enriched, pos1-depleted, primarily LF-occupying "formula words." B-dialect LF-formula ranking: s > y > d in LF-enrichment strength. y differs from d: d is LL-enriched (A=1.68×, B=1.37×) while y is LL-DEPLETED (A=0.61×, B=0.64×); y is more purely a "LF-only" class in terms of LL behavior. y also differs from s: B s is more strongly LF-concentrated (4.73×) and more MID-depleted (0.41× vs y's 0.64×). Architecture: the three B LF-formula classes (s, y, d) have distinct outgoing behaviors but share LF-concentration, making them all "line-opener" words that rarely appear at non-LF positions.

### Finding 2: y Self-Succession Is the STRONGEST of All Characterized Onset Classes (A=2.06×/MID=3.15×; B=2.71×/MID=2.67×) — MID-Concentrated in A, Pan-Positional in B
