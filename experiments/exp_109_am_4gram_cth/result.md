## H212: REFUTED — Voynichese Has Essentially NO Cross-Folio 4-gram Repetition; Only 1 of 16,210 Unique 4-grams Repeats Across 2 Folios (0.006%); 5-grams: 0 Cross-Folio; Text Is Compositionally Unique Per Folio

### By Section

| Section | Unique 4-grams | Cross-folio |

**No section other than cosmo has ANY cross-folio 4-gram repetition.** The single cosmo instance ("ol shedy qokedy qokeedy") is likely a formulaic phrase in the cosmo section rather than systematic repetition.

### Implications

The near-complete absence of 4-gram repetition across folios is consistent with:
1. **Heaps' law β=0.66-0.74** (F111): high vocabulary growth rate means each folio adds new words
2. **High Zipf-Mandelbrot diversity** (F112): no single dominant "the"-equivalent word sequence
3. **Section-specific vocabulary** (F112): 72-79% of tokens are section-specific content, generating unique sequences per folio

**This REFUTES the hypothesis that Voynichese contains formulaic 4-gram sequences repeated across the manuscript.** Unlike some constructed languages or cipher texts with repetitive structures, Voynichese generates essentially unique word sequences for each folio.

### H212 Verdict: REFUTED — Only 1 of 16,210 Unique 4-grams Repeats Across 2 Folios (0.006%); 5-grams: 0 Cross-Folio; Voynichese Text Sequences Are Compositionally Unique Per Folio, Consistent With Heaps' β=0.66-0.74 and 72-79% Section-Specific Vocabulary; No Systematic Formulaic Repetition at 4-Word Level

### cth- Linguistic Interpretation

cth- is a **GALLOWS-EXTENDED A-DIALECT CLOSER**:
- Structure: cth (gallows cluster) + vowel/y-initial suffix

- Position: line-closer (LLast=25-26%)
- Paradigm: strongly A-type (-y/-or/-ol dominant, -edy/-eedy suppressed)
- Section: herbal and pharma exclusively
- NOT a generative prefix parallel to qo/o — instead, a morphophonologically constrained form appearing only in A-dialect closing positions

The cth- cluster represents the MOST extreme manifestation of the A-paradigm: same positional role as d-onset (closer) but even more A-exclusive and with more strongly A-paradigm suffixes.

## Combined Finding (F116)

**H211 CONFIRMED PARTIAL (F116)**: The -am closed class is dialect-neutral overall (A/B=1.00×) but dialect-SPLIT within: A-onset -am words (dam=3.1×, cham=3.5×, sam=11.6×) are A-dominant; qo/o-onset -am words (qokam=0.3×, ram=0.2×) are B-dominant. The -am SUFFIX is dialect-neutral; the ONSET determines dialect identity. All -am words are line-closers (LL=60-100%). cosmo B suppresses -am class (6.83‰ vs 22-41‰). oly/oldy is B-preferred (2.50× B/A).

**H212 REFUTED (F116)**: Voynichese has essentially ZERO cross-folio 4-gram repetition (1/16,210 unique 4-grams, 0.006%). 5-grams: 0. Consistent with Heaps' β=0.66-0.74 (high lexical diversity) and 72-79% section-specific vocabulary. Voynichese generates compositionally unique sequences per folio — no evidence of formulaic repetition at 4+ word level.

**H213 CONFIRMED (F116)**: cth- is the most A-exclusive onset class found (14× A/B ratio). It is a GALLOWS-EXTENDED A-DIALECT CLOSER: line-closing (LLast=25-26%), A-paradigm suffix dominant (enriches -y/-or/-ol; suppresses -edy/-eedy), concentrated in herbal/pharma sections (2-38‰ range). NOT a parallel to qo-/o- prefix systems (only 17-24% stem overlap). cth- represents the extreme A-end of the onset-class paradigm spectrum.

**KEY FINDING (F116)**: The Voynichese prefix/onset system has a DIALECT POLARITY AXIS:
- **Extreme A-pole**: cth- (14× A-ratio, LLast=26%, herbal/pharma)
- **Strong A-pole**: d-onset (3-5× A-ratio in A-dominant sections, LLast=26%)
- **Neutral/paradigm-base**: ch/sh/qo (moderate A or B depending on suffix)
- **Strong B-pole**: s-onset (1.5-3× B-ratio in B sections, LFirst=19%)
- **Extreme B-pole**: (not yet found — may be in stars/cosmo-specific classes)

The A/B paradigm split (F114) is thus not binary but GRADED, with cth- at the A extreme and s-onset shifted toward B. The onset class encodes BOTH positional role (opener/closer) AND dialect polarity on a single axis.
