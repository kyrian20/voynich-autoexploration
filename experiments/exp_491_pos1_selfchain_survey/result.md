## 3. Cross-Class Routing vs Self-Chaining at pos1

The key structural insight: pos1 is the **cross-class routing** position in A herbal_a, competing with self-chaining.

**Classes with dominant cross-class routing at pos1:**
- ot-LF: y at pos1 (CONFIRMED F456, E=1.83×/N=11) — ot-SELF at pos1 is **DEPLETED** (E=0.43×)
- ok-LF: y at pos1 (CONFIRMED F455, E=1.62×/N=12 approximately from obs=12) — but ok-SELF is also elevated (E=1.80×/N=6, sub-threshold)
- d-LF: ot at pos1 (CONFIRMED F453) — d-SELF at pos1 E=1.34× (below threshold)

**Classes where self-chaining dominates pos1:**
- ch: ch-self CONFIRMED (F473, E=1.58×/N=30) + sch dir
- y: y-self CONFIRMED (F472, E=2.13×/N=11)
- sh: sh-self dir (E=2.55×/N=9)
- s: s-self dir (E=6.07×/N=7)
- qot: qot-self dir (E=3.04×/N=7)

**Pattern:** ot, ok, d use pos1 for CROSS-CLASS onset routing (gateway words that define the line type). ch, y, sh, s, qot use pos1 for SELF-CHAINING (the first word echoes the terminal word class).

**pos1 grammar summary:** pos1 serves two distinct functions in A herbal_a lines:
1. **Cross-class opener** (for d, ok, ot): the first word belongs to a DIFFERENT onset class than the LF, establishing the line's "formula type"
2. **Self-chaining opener** (for ch, y, sh, qot, s): the first word echoes the LF onset class

The distinction is correlated with the onset-class type: d/ok/ot (gallows and bench compounds) use cross-class pos1; ch/y/sh/s (different structural classes) self-chain at pos1. This suggests pos1 distinguishes two distinct line-composition strategies in A herbal_a.

**Extrapolation:** "pos1 NOT universal" is supported by ot being DEPLETED and d/o/ok being below threshold. y being confirmed at pos1 is an unexpected result (not in prior findings). ch confirmed at pos1 is consistent with ch dominating at both positions.

## Conclusions

**F472 ASSIGNED: A herbal_a y-LF → y-self at pos1 (E=2.13×/N=11, CONFIRMED).** y self-chains at both pos1 AND pos2. y-LF lines systematically begin and contain y-onset words at both positions.

**F473 ASSIGNED: A herbal_a ch-LF → ch-self at pos1 (E=1.58×/N=30, CONFIRMED).** ch self-chains at BOTH pos1 (new) and pos2 (F461). ch is the only onset class with confirmed self-chaining at every position tested (pos1, pos2, and near-threshold at pen per F454 retraction).

**H_new_BP ANSWERED:** pos1 is NOT universal in A herbal_a (unlike pos2). Two confirmed pos1 self-chainers (ch, y); several directional (sh, qot, s). ot-LF pos1 is DEPLETED — cross-class routing (y) displaces self-chaining. d, ok, cth, o below threshold at pos1 (dominated by cross-class routing or flat). The pos1 position serves dual functions: cross-class opener (for d/ok/ot) vs self-chaining opener (for ch/y/sh), distinguishing two line-composition types in A herbal_a.
