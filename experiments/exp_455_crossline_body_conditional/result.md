## Interpretation

**H_new_R CONFIRMED: Body-conditional rules extend cross-line, but with DIFFERENT routing targets than within-line.**

1. **Within-line vs cross-line routing differs for qok+a-body:**
   - Within MID (F439): qok+a-body → sh elevated (E=1.73×)
   - Cross-line LL→LF (this exp): qok+a-body → s elevated (E=1.76×), sh NOT elevated
   - The same body class (qok+aiin) routes to sh in within-line grammar but to s in cross-line grammar. This is a context-dependent routing where the grammatical function of the body class changes by position (MID vs LL).

2. **Body class is the specific driver of within-class cross-line couplings:**
   - B d→sh cross-line (E=1.79×, F438): driven by d+a-body (daiin E=1.99×)
   - B d→qok cross-line (E=1.56×, F438): also driven by d+a-body (daiin E=1.68×)
   - B qok→s cross-line (E=1.51×, F438): driven by qok+a-body (qokain/qokaiin E=1.76×)
   - B s→s cross-line (E=2.20×, F438): driven by s+a-body (saiin E=2.60×)
   - F438's cross-line couplings are in each case concentrated in the aiin-body subclass of the LL word

3. **A ch→ch cross-line (F438: A=1.57×/N=28): driven by ch+e-body (chedy/cheey E=2.79×/N=13).** In A, the edy-body ch words (che- prefix) drive cross-line ch→ch self-succession, parallel to F439's finding that edy-body words drive within-line self-succession.

4. **General principle: suffix body determines cross-line grammatical role.** aiin-body words (d, s, qok with aiin suffix) are cross-line "bridge" words — they cause successive-line connections to sh, s, or qok onset classes. edy-body words (ch, qok with edy suffix) are cross-line "self-replicating" words — they maintain same-class succession at cross-line. This extends F439's two-level grammar to the CROSS-LINE domain.

5. **Interpretation of B qok+a-body at LL → s (not sh) cross-line:**
   The within-line rule qok+a-body → sh operates in MID position because sh is a common MID successor in within-line grammar. At LL, the grammar transitions to cross-line territory where F438's qok→s (1.51×) rule dominates. The qok+a-body subclass drives the qok→s cross-line coupling more strongly (1.76× vs 1.51× overall) while also contributing to qok→qok cross-line (1.60×).

## Conclusion

H_new_R CONFIRMED: Body-conditional rules extend cross-line. **F441 (new finding):** The F438 cross-line couplings are specifically driven by aiin-body LL words: B d+aiin→sh CL E=1.99×(N=14), B qok+aiin→s CL E=1.76×(N=17), B d+aiin→qok CL E=1.68×(N=22), B s+aiin→s CL E=2.60×(N=7), A ch+edy→ch CL E=2.79×(N=13). aiin-body LL words are cross-line bridge words (routing to diverse subsequent-line LF classes); edy-body LL words drive same-class cross-line self-succession. The within-line body-conditional rules (F439) differ from cross-line rules by position: qok+a-body routes to sh within-line (F439) but to s cross-line (F441). Body class encodes grammatical function that persists across line boundaries with context-dependent routing.
