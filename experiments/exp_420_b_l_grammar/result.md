## Interpretation

**l-LF is a minor B class (N=63) with a specific formula routing:** l→ch at pos1 is near-baseline (E=0.92×), but given l→ch, pos2=qok is strongly enriched (E=2.96×/N=5). This confirms the l→ch→qok formula type (F421) as a ROUTING rule (not a selection rule): l-LF does not preferentially select ch at pos1, but when ch occurs at pos1 in l-LF lines, the line routes to qok at pos2 with high probability.

**l self-succession is POSITION-DEPENDENT**: E=3.03× at pos1→pos2 but only 1.87× at LF→pos1 and 1.99× in MID. The pos1→pos2 stickiness suggests that l-onset words cluster especially in the pos1-pos2 slot of B lines. This could reflect a specific word-pair or doublet construction where two l-onset words appear at the content slot (positions 1-2 of the line).

**Pharma section dominance**: l-dense lines (≥3 l-onset words) are 3.5% of pharma cont lines vs 0.3-1.2% in other sections. The longest l-chains (len=3-4) are ALL in pharma folios (f111-116). This identifies a section-grammar property: l-onset word clustering is a pharma-section phenomenon. The 'lk-' compound words (lkaiin, lkchey, lkain) dominate the l-runs — these are l+k body compounds, possibly pharmaceutical/recipe terminology.

**l is a pharma-section content class** with a specific grammar rule (l→ch→qok routing). The broad l self-succession (E=2.35× overall) is driven primarily by this pharma-section clustering of 'lk-' compound words.
