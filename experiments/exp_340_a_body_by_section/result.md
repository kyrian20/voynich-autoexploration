# exp_340: A-Dialect {ol,or} Body Vocabulary Enrichment by Section (H543)

**Date:** 2026-04-11
**Hypothesis:** H543 — The A-dialect universal {aiin→ol} shift (F340) is section-specific rather than uniform; bio section may drive the enrichment.
**Result:** H543 PARTIALLY CONFIRMED. The {ol,or} enrichment IS section-dependent: A-dialect pharma shows extreme ol/or dominance (17.88×), herbal moderate (3.98×), astro elevated (8.50×), cosmo near-balanced (1.25×). A-dialect bio section is NOT PRESENT in this corpus (bio folios classify as B-dialect). The most extreme section is pharma, not bio. B-dialect bio section has high aiin/ain usage (qokain=160 in bio vs 100 in text), confirming bio is a B-dialect section with strong aiin-body template usage.

## Findings

### Finding 1: A-dialect pharma section drives extreme ol/or enrichment (ratio=17.88×)

Pharma section shows the most extreme {ol,or}/{aiin,ain} imbalance: 5.58% ol/or vs only 0.31% aiin/ain (ratio=17.88×). chol=42, chor=26, qokol=24 are substantial counts in pharma. Template-onset {aiin,ain} forms are nearly absent from A-dialect pharma. Pharma section in A-dialect appears to use ol/or body as the primary body vocabulary for template onsets.

### Finding 2: A-dialect herbal section shows moderate ol/or dominance (3.98×)

Herbal section has the largest absolute counts (chol=184, chor=120) and ratio=3.98×. Herbal still has significant aiin/ain usage (chaiin=31, okaiin=31, qokaiin=23) so it's not as extreme as pharma. The herbal section is where most of the A-dialect corpus lives (total=6306 words).

### Finding 3: A-dialect cosmo section is balanced (ratio=1.25×)

The cosmo section shows roughly balanced ol/or and aiin/ain usage (1.45% vs 1.16%). This is qualitatively different from all other A-dialect sections. Cosmo folios may have a different scribal pattern that preserves aiin-body usage in template onsets.

### Finding 4: B-dialect bio section is aiin/ain dominant (high contrast with A-dialect)

B-dialect bio section: qokain=160, qokaiin=87 (very high) vs chol=12, chor=0 (very low). B-dialect bio is the most aiin/ain-concentrated section for template onsets. This contrasts with A-dialect herbal/pharma (ol/or-concentrated). The bio section appears in B-dialect only (bio folios f75-f84 classify as B-dialect in this corpus).

---

## Interpretation

The A-dialect {aiin→ol} shift is not uniform — it is most extreme in pharma and herbal sections. The cosmo section shows near-balance, suggesting that section-level scribal practice differs. This is consistent with the working model that different VMS sections may reflect different compositional practices or scribal conventions.
