## 1. H324: d@LF→cth@second DRIVEN BY daiin (38%) AND d+ch COMPOUNDS (4.42×)

| dar, dor, dair, dol | 12, 10, 9, 8 | 0 each | 0% | 0× |

**daiin contributes 8/21 cases (38%) — it is the single largest contributor.** However, d+ch compound line-openers (dchor, dchol, dchey) have even higher enrichment (4.42× each), contributing 4/21 (19%) collectively. The enrichment is NOT exclusively driven by daiin — it's distributed across multiple d-LF word types. Pure d-stem words without prefixes (dar, dor, dair, dol) contribute 0 cases, suggesting the cth@second enrichment requires the d-opener to itself be semantically "connected" to the cth-word class.

**Pre-registration partially confirmed**: daiin is the largest contributor (38%), but d+ch compounds are also strongly enriched.
