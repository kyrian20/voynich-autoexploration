**H_new_CO ANSWERED:** A cosmo has THREE confirmed pen rules (F486/F487/F488) — richer than expected given A cosmo's relatively flat pos2 grammar (0 confirmed rules, exp_499). The pen grammar concentrates in specific LF classes (ch, d, o) with section-specific targets:
