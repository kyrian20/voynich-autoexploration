## Findings

### Finding 1: s Is a PURE LF-FORMULA CLASS — B s LF=4.73× Is the Strongest LF-Enrichment of Any Characterized Onset Class

A s: LF=2.22×, pos1=0.31×, MID=0.52×, LL=1.49×. B s: LF=4.73×, pos1=0.32×, MID=0.41×, pre_LL=0.46×, LL=0.74×. The s onset class is the most position-restricted class characterized: in B, over 60% of all s tokens appear at LF despite s being only 2.4% of all tokens. B s LF=4.73× surpasses B d LF=2.29× (F385) to become the largest LF enrichment factor of any characterized onset class. s differs from d in LL profile: d is LL-enriched (A=1.68×, B=1.37×) while B s is LL-DEPLETED (0.74×), and A s has modest LL enrichment (1.49×) that is much weaker than A d's 1.68×. Architecture: s is a pure "line-opening word" — it occupies LF and essentially nothing else. s→sh and s→ch at LF→pos1 (B=2.47×, A=1.85×/1.55×) confirm that the LF-formula mechanism takes s at LF as input and produces ch/sh at pos1 as output.

### Finding 2: B s→sh LF=2.47×(N=62/260) — Confirms F376 Exactly; Most Concentrated B s Outgoing Rule

B s→sh: LF→pos1=2.47×(N=62/260), MID=1.15×(near-baseline), pre_LL=0.43×(depleted). Overall E=1.98×. This exactly confirms F376 (B s→sh LF-position-specific E=2.47×, N=62). Top B s→sh pairs: saiin→shedy(3), sain→shedy(3), saiin→shey(3), sol→sheedy(2). The B s→sh LF rule is the dominant B s outgoing bigram: 15.8% of all B s LF outgoing transitions lead to sh at pos1. Architecture: when B s is the LF word, it preferentially opens with a sh-class word at pos1. This is the B-dialect version of the LF-formula mechanism: s→sh at LF is a specific formula pattern.

### Finding 3: B s→ol Is Pan-Positional (LF=1.58×, MID=1.65×, pre_LL=4.47×) — s→ol Rule Extends Beyond pre_LL, Confirms B LL Routing

B s→ol: LF→pos1=1.58×(N=23/260), MID_inner=1.65×(N=10/108), pre_LL→LL=4.47×(N=6/24). Overall E=1.78×. F376 documented B s→ol pre_LL concentrated; the new finding is that s→ol is elevated at LF and MID as well (both ~1.6×). The dominant signal is pre_LL (4.47×) but s→ol is a general s outgoing coupling, not only a pre_LL rule. Top B s→ol pairs: sain→ol(4), sar→ol(3), saiin→olcheey(1), sar→oly(1). Updated B LL routing: s at pre_LL → ol at LL E=2.98×(6/24) confirmed (consistent with F376/F379 s→ol routing). B s at pre_LL → qok=0.00×(absent), r=0.00×(absent), d=0.45×(depleted) — s pre_LL routing is specifically to ol.

### Finding 4: B s→cth=0.00× (Categorical Absence, 0/392) — s and cth Are Mutually Exclusive in B

B s→cth: 0 occurrences out of 392 s-outgoing bigrams (E=0.00×). This is a categorical mutual exclusion: in B-dialect, s-onset words NEVER precede cth-onset words. This parallels B r→s=0.00× (F382) — another categorical absence in B. Architecture: B s and cth are exclusive: s is a LF-formula class (almost never at MID/pos2), while cth may appear at MID; but even so the complete absence is striking. A s→cth: E=0.73× (near-baseline, not categorical in A, N=6/175). The B s↔cth mutual exclusion is dialect-specific.

### Finding 5: B s→d LF=0.29× (Strongly Depleted) — Two LF-Formula Classes Do Not Co-Occur at LF→pos1

B s→d: LF→pos1=0.29×(N=4/260), MID=1.38×(N=8/108). Overall E=0.62×. When B s is at LF, it very rarely precedes d at pos1 (only 4/260 = 1.5% of outgoing, baseline=5.4%). This makes architectural sense: B d is also a LF-formula class (LF=2.29×, F385), so d is preferentially AT LF not at pos1. When s is at LF, the pos1 slot is filled by ch/sh/ol (the "second-position response classes"), not by another LF-formula word (d). Contrast with B s→sh LF=2.47× and B s→ch LF=1.11× (near-baseline). Architecture: the mutual LF-preference of s and d creates depletion at s→d LF→pos1. This mirrors the more general pattern that LF-formula classes (d, s, qot in B?) do not co-occur at LF+pos1.

### Finding 6: B s Self-Succession Suppressed (E=0.69×, MID=0.00×, pre_LL=0.00×) — s Does Not Chain

B s→s: overall=0.69×(depleted); LF→pos1=1.05×(flat), MID=0.00×(absent), pre_LL=0.00×(absent). Total B s→s hits: 3 tokens (all at LF→pos1). B s does not self-chain at any non-LF position. Compare: B d has strong self-succession at pre_LL (E=3.63×, F385); A ol has pan-positional self-succession (F381). s is the clearest example of a class where near-zero non-LF occurrence prevents self-chaining.
