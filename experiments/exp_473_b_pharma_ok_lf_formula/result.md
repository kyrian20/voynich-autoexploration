## Interpretation

**H_new_AO ANSWERED (all directional):**

**B pharma ok-LF → l at pos1 (E=2.37×/N=8, directional)** is the main finding. This connects to the pharma l-chain structure (F423: l→ch→qok formula type, l-dense lines in pharma). The routing chain:

ok[LF] → l[pos1] → ?[pos2] → ... 

If pos2 after l at pos1 follows F423's routing (l→ch→qok), the full sequence would be: ok[LF] → l[pos1] → ch[pos2] → qok[pos3]. This would be a pharma-specific 4-onset sequence. The pos2 shows ok=2.01×(N=7) — ok self-succession skipping pos1 — suggesting an alternative route: ok[LF] → [l or qok][pos1] → ok[pos2]. Two competing routing patterns:
1. ok→l→ch→qok (extending F423 from l-LF to ok-LF starting the sequence)
2. ok→[var]→ok (ok-skip-self at pos2)

**B pharma ok-LF is pharma-concentrated** (B herbal: N=3 ok-LF vs pharma: N=44). This is consistent with F447's pharma-specific ok-LF elevation and the pharma section grammar layer.

**ok vocabulary structure**: The top ok-LF words (okeey/okaiin/okain/okeedy) parallel qok-LF word structure (qokeedy/qokaiin, F451). B pharma ok may have a body-conditional routing rule analogous to B qok+edy/aiin bifurcation (F451). This is worth testing (H_new_AQ).

**B pharma y-LF confirms F430**: y→o at pos2 (E=2.02×/N=8) is the pharma y-formula routing reproduced from existing findings.

**No confirmed formula type for B pharma ok-LF** (all N<10 at any single position). The l at pos1 (N=8) is the strongest directional signal.
