## Combined Finding (F103)

**H175 STRONGLY CONFIRMED**: p+ch is para-starter in BOTH A (26.9% PS) and B (11.6% PS). y+ch, d+ch are line-openers in BOTH (0% PS, 75-84% LFirst). bare_ch is mid-line in BOTH. MORPHOLOGICAL CLASS determines positional role cross-dialectally. The gallows-demotion model is UNIVERSAL.

**H176 CONFIRMED**: B bare_k enrichment (9.91×) is a contrast effect — bare_k is mostly mid-line (92%) but rare at within-para line-first (0.78%), creating high ratio. Absolute PS count is only 8 tokens.

**H177 STRONGLY CONFIRMED (F103) — MASTER UNIFIED GRAMMAR**: The positional grammar is determined by PREFIX TYPE, not by dialect:
- **{p, f} prefix on any stem** → PARA-LEVEL markers (A: 21-56% PS; B: 9-15% PS)
- **{y, d} prefix on any stem** → LINE-OPENERS (PS=0%, LFirst=50-96%)
- **{qo} prefix on gallows OR bare non-gallows** → MID-LINE (PS≈0%, LFirst<20%)

**The PREFIX TYPE IS THE POSITIONAL MORPHEME** in Voynichese. Adding p or f-prefix to a word makes it a paragraph marker. Adding y or d-prefix makes it a within-para line-opener. Adding qo-prefix makes it mid-line. The underlying STEM (ch, sh, k, t) determines content but not position. **Position is entirely determined by prefix choice.**

This is structurally identical to positional particles in topic-prominent languages (Japanese wa/ga, Turkish postpositions) — except here the positional markers are PREFIXES not separate words.
