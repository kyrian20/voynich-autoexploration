## Interpretation

**H_new_AL ANSWERED — A herbal_a qot-LF ≠ B astro qot-LF routing:**

The two qot-LF grammars differ structurally:
- **B astro qot-LF**: ch→pos1(1.62×), ot→LL(3.15×,dir) — the B astro qot/ot spatial pair
- **A herbal_a qot-LF**: no strong pos1 routing; cth→pos2(2.32×,dir); d→LL(1.44×,N=24)

**NEW (directional): A herbal_a qot-LF → cth at pos2 (E=2.32×/N=8)**

This is consistent with the A-dialect within-line formula "qot→ch→cth" (F406: E=2.60×/N=4 at positions pos1→pos2→pos3). The qot-LF line structure would place this formula starting at LF: qot[LF] → ch[pos1] → cth[pos2]. If this is the mechanism:
- 22/59 qot-LF lines have ch at pos1 (baseline, E=1.10× — not elevated overall)
- But conditional on ch at pos1: cth at pos2 may be selectively elevated (not tested here)
- The 8 cth-at-pos2 cases would predominantly come from qot+ch+cth triples

This interpretation connects F406 (A formula type within-line) to the LF-formula context: when qot is the LF class, the qot→ch→cth within-line formula activates at the LF→pos1→pos2 positions.

**A herbal_a qot-LF LL: d-class dominates** (dy/daiin/dar at LL). This is NOT consistent with qot-LF being a "formula-typed" line in the B sense (where formula types have ch or ot at LL). Instead, A herbal_a qot-LF lines end with d-class words — the same d class that begins the most common A herbal_a LF.

**A/B LF-class inversion at LL in A herbal_a:**
- d-LF(most common) → ch dominant at LL (1.39×/N=35)
- qot-LF(6th most common) → d dominant at LL (1.44×/N=24)
- This is an A herbal_a-level bracket inversion: d opens / qot opens, and ch terminates d-lines while d terminates qot-lines. A complementary LL routing structure.

**A herbal_a qot-LF lacks a confirmed formula type** — no pos1 routing at E≥2×/N≥5. The qot→ch→cth finding (F406) is a within-line formula type at positions pos1-pos2-pos3, not a LF-class formula at LF-pos1-pos2. The cth at pos2 finding (E=2.32×/N=8) is directional only.
