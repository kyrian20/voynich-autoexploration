# H030 Sub-Test: Are Other Hand 2 Folios Part of the Sequence?

## Test
Searched all 46 Hand 2 folios in ZL3b for L0 entries (any prefix variant).

## Result
**Only f75v and f76r have L0 entries among all Hand 2 folios.**

The 44 other Hand 2 folios (f26r/v, f31r/v, f33-34, f39-40, f43, f46, f50, f55, f75r, f76v, f77-84, f85-86) have zero L0 line markers.

## Implication for H030
The f75v (6 markers) and f76r (9 markers) sequences are the COMPLETE set of Hand 2 line-marker data. There are no additional folios extending the sequence.

**H030 FALSIFIED** as a multi-folio sequence hypothesis. The combined character inventory {d, k, l, o, q, r, s} = 7 distinct chars is the complete Hand 2 line-marker vocabulary across both folios.

This makes f75v and f76r a paired two-folio line-marker group within the biological section (f75v is f75 verso, f76r is f76 recto — they are the recto/verso of adjacent folios in the quire, i.e., f75v|f76r are the inner opening of a bifolium).
