## 2. Five Structural Classes at LF

### 2a. INVARIANT LF+ — Enriched at LF in BOTH Dialects

{p, t, f, s, y} — all show LF-enrichment in continuation lines across dialects:
- **Gallows sub-group**: p(A=3.53×/B=5.85×), t(A=2.43×/B=3.58×), f(A=2.69×/B=1.67×)
- **Content sub-group**: s(A=2.22×/B=4.73×), y(A=2.28×/B=3.52×)

All five classes are consistently LF-positioned in CONTINUATION lines (not just folio-opener lines). This is important: the three-register model established p/t as folio-opener LF formulas, but this table shows p/t are also LF-enriched within continuation lines.

### 2b. INVARIANT LF- — Depleted at LF in BOTH Dialects

{ch, ol, r} — all strongly depleted at LF:
- ch: A=0.39×, B=0.25× — strongest LF depletion; pos1-enriched response class
- r: A=0.27×, B=0.30× — LL-terminal class
- ol: A=0.64×, B=0.58× — LL-enriched class

### 2c. A>B Inversion (A LF-enriched, B LF-depleted)

**qot only**: A=1.65×, B=0.51× (3.2× ratio between dialects) — the clearest cross-dialect LF inversion.

### 2d. B>A Contrast (A LF-depleted, B LF-enriched or neutral)

**cth**: A=0.13×(most LF-depleted of all classes), B=1.16×(near-neutral). 8.9× ratio. cth is a "mid-to-end" class in A (F388) but appears near-LF-neutral in B.

**d**: A=1.15×(mild), B=2.29×(strong) — d is more strongly LF-formula in B than A. Not a true inversion (A is >1.0) but a large magnitude difference.

### 2e. Template Classes — Graded A>B LF Depletion

ot: A=0.91×, B=0.33× — B ot is strongly LF-depleted, A ot is near-neutral.
ok: A=0.81×, B=0.49× — similar pattern.
qok: A=1.09×, B=0.79× — A qok slightly above neutral, B below.

These template classes {ok, ot, qok} all show more LF-depletion in B than A.

## 3. k Anomaly: The Gallows Exception

k (gallows class) is NOT LF-enriched in continuation lines: A=0.80×, B=0.65×. This contrasts with p(A=3.53×), t(A=2.43×), f(A=2.69×) all showing strong LF-enrichment.

k is a large class (A=314 tokens/3.7% vs p=66/0.8%) and appears throughout lines. The F364 finding clarifies: k is sh-biased at LF→pos1 and ch-biased at MID/pre_LL — k appears at multiple positions. By contrast, p-onset words appear nearly exclusively at LF (41/66=62% of p tokens are at LF in A continuation lines). k is a position-general gallows class; p/t/f are more LF-specific.

---

## 4. Position-Selective vs Position-General Classes

**Highly position-specific (LF-concentrated):**
- A: p(62% at LF), y(40%), s(39%), t(43%), f(47%)
- B: p(64% at LF), s(61%), y(45%), t(58%)

**Widely distributed:**
- k, ok, ot, sh — spread across positions
- d — large class, appears at all positions
