## H329: [daiin...dam@LL] LINE STRUCTURE

**Immediate [daiin][dam] bigrams: 4 cases in A-dialect**:
1. `f22 [herbal]: qotchy cthy qokol daiin dam` (len=5 — daiin at penultimate, immediately before dam)
2. `f23 [herbal]: to ar chor daiin chk,dain otchy lolchor daiin dam okchol dain,g` (long line; daiin dam at end)
3. `f32 [herbal]: qo,ar daiin dam` (len=3 — short line)
4. `f89 [pharma]: qeaiin cheyl seey qotey qokeeol daiin daiin dam` (len=8 — has **double daiin** before dam: `daiin daiin dam`)

**f89 has [daiin daiin dam]** — two consecutive daiin tokens followed by dam. This is the only [daiin daiin] consecutive pair in the dataset followed by dam. The `daiin daiin` sequence at penultimate-antepenultimate is itself interesting (daiin self-succession).

**None of the [daiin dam] lines are simple 2-word lines** — all have at least 3 words (shortest: `qo,ar daiin dam` at len=3). The [daiin][dam] immediate bigram is not a minimal formula; daiin precedes dam only after a preceding clause structure.

**Among 23 A-dialect dam@LL lines**: 3 have daiin as immediate penultimate (lines 1,3,4 above are immediate; f23 is also immediate). So 4/23=17.4% of dam@LL lines have [daiin][dam] immediately — enrichment 17.4%/4.04%=4.31× for daiin specifically as immediate penultimate before dam. This is stronger than the 3.23× for daiin anywhere as penultimate (from F169).

1. **[ot][ot]=1.84× (n=26)**: ot-words moderately self-chain. No dominant single [ot-word][ot-word] formula (otaiin→otaiin=2× is the only repeat). The enrichment is real but not a specific fixed phrase — it reflects a general tendency for ot-words to appear in clusters.
