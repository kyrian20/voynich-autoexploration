# exp_367: Onset-Class Self-Succession Survey (H580)

**Date:** 2026-04-11
**Hypothesis:** H580 — Onset-class self-succession (X→X enrichment) is a universal property of ALL template onset classes, not specific to ok/qok/d. Prediction: ch/sh/ot/ol/y/s also show X→X E > 1.2× in continuation lines. Gallows classes {k, t, f} may not show self-succession.
**Result:** H580 CONFIRMED. Self-succession is UNIVERSAL among template onset classes: all of {ot, y, ok, ol, qok, sh, d, ch, s, qot} show E > 1.2× in A-dialect continuation lines. Only gallows classes {k, t, f} show E ≤ 1.0×. Critical dialect contrast: B-dialect ch→ch (0.94×) and sh→sh (0.95×) are NEAR BASELINE (vs A ch=1.22×, sh=1.41×) — B-dialect ch↔sh avoidance (F328) suppresses these classes' self-succession. New: d→cth A E=1.62× (70 pairs); sh→t A E=2.04× (30 pairs). Continuation-line bigram matrix differs from F326 all-lines values.

## Findings

### Finding 1: Self-Succession Is Universal Among Template Onset Classes; Gallows Classes {k, t, f} Are the Exception

All template classes (ch/sh/qok/ok/ot/ol/d/y/s/qot) show E > 1.2× in A-dialect. Only gallows classes {k, t, f} fail to show self-succession (k=0.87×, t=0.67×, f=0.00×). This is a structural distinction: template-class words cluster by onset class; gallows-class words do not repeat adjacent to themselves. The pattern is: gallows words appear as isolated structural markers (folio-opener LF, line-opener markers), not in runs, while template words form succession chains.

### Finding 2: B-Dialect ch→ch and sh→sh Are Near-Baseline (0.94×/0.95×) vs A-Dialect Elevated (1.22×/1.41×) — B ch↔sh Avoidance Suppresses Cross-Succession, Not Self-Succession (a Nuance of F328)

In B-dialect, ch→ch and sh→sh are both near 1.0×, while in A-dialect both are elevated (1.22×/1.41×). Crucially, B-dialect ch→sh=0.60× and sh→ch=0.57× (strongly depleted). The B-dialect ch↔sh avoidance is cross-class only (ch→sh and sh→ch depleted) — self-succession of each class is separately neutral. In A-dialect, the weaker/unidirectional ch↔sh avoidance (F338: only sh→ch persists, ch→sh normalizes) allows both ch and sh to sustain self-succession chains. This refines F328: the B-dialect avoidance is a ch↔sh CROSS-COUPLING avoidance, not a general ch/sh exclusion.

### Finding 3: qot→qot Is the Strongest Well-Supported Self-Succession in A-Dialect (E=2.60×, N=14)

qot-onset has the highest self-succession E in A-dialect (E=2.60×, N=14). Although N=14 is moderate, this is consistent with qot's known properties: qot-onset words tend to appear in clusters at specific positions (qot-onset is enriched at LF: E=2.09× per F368). qot→qot succession (6.5% of qot transitions → another qot) suggests qot-class words form multi-word onset sequences. In B-dialect, qot→qot E=2.06× (N=56) — cross-dialect confirmation.

### Finding 4: ot→ot Cross-Dialect (A=1.94×, B=1.93×) — Highest Robust Cross-Dialect Self-Succession

ot-onset self-succession is nearly identical in both dialects (A=1.94×/N=25, B=1.93×/N=138). The B-dialect value is well-supported (N=138). ot-class words cluster together regardless of dialect context. ot is a template-onset class and its self-succession is the most cross-dialectally stable finding in this experiment.

### Finding 5: New A-Dialect Cross-Onset Rules — d→cth E=1.62× (N=70) and sh→t E=2.04× (N=30)

Two new cross-onset bigram rules in A-dialect:
1. **d→cth E=1.62×** (N=70): d-onset words are followed by cth-onset words above baseline. This is a d→cth coupling not previously documented. (In F326's 7×7 matrix, cth was merged into the "other" category.)
2. **sh→t E=2.04×** (N=30): sh-onset followed by t-onset (gallows class?) above baseline. The "t-onset" class in the onset order captures words starting with 't' but not preceded by q/c/o — these include tcheor, tchar, ty-compounds. This sh→t-compound rule is new.
Both require verification with larger N before claiming significance, but N=70 for d→cth is adequate.

Only gallows {k, t, f} do not show self-succession. B-dialect confirms universality except: B ch→ch=0.94× and sh→sh=0.95× (near baseline) — the B ch↔sh bidirectional avoidance suppresses cross-coupling but not self-succession (a nuance of F328). Cross-dialect self-succession: ot (1.94×/1.93×) and y (1.92×/2.71×) are the strongest. New: d→cth A E=1.62× (N=70). Architecture implication: ALL template classes need onset-class memory modeling, not just ok/qok/d. The gallows class {k/t/f} is the structural exception — no self-succession.
