# exp_423: B d→ch→l Section Distribution

**Hypothesis (H645):** d→ch→l=2.16×(N=12) formula type — is it pharma-section-specific?

**Method:** B cont lines: section distribution of all d→ch lines vs d→ch→l lines vs d→sh→l lines.

---

## Results

| Subset | herbal | astro | bio | pharma |
|--------|--------|-------|-----|--------|
| B cont lines overall | 12% | 33% | 10% | 45% |
| d→ch lines (N=80) | 17% | 20% | 11% | 51% |
| d→ch→l lines (N=12) | 8% | 33% | 0% | 58% |
| d→sh→l lines (N=5) | 0% | 20% | 0% | 80% |

- d→ch→l pharma=7/12(58%) vs d→ch overall pharma=41/80(51%) — modest enrichment (E≈1.1×, directional).
- d→sh→l pharma=4/5(80%) vs d→sh overall: more concentrated but N=5 directional.
- Astro section has 4/12 d→ch→l lines (33%) — unexpectedly high vs astro's 20% share of d→ch lines.

Notable: f76/42 has a d→ch→l→d→ch→l pattern (l at pos2 AND pos5) — d-ch-l triplet repeats.

---

## Interpretation

**d→ch→l is NOT strongly pharma-specific** (E≈1.1× in pharma vs baseline). The astro section has a notable concentration (33% of d→ch→l vs 20% of d→ch overall). The pharma-section l-clustering (F423) is driven primarily by l-LF lines and 'lk-' compounds in long runs, NOT by d→ch→l specifically. The d→ch→l formula type (E=2.16×/N=12) is a line-level grammar rule that operates across multiple sections.

---

## Conclusion

H645 ANSWERED (negative). d→ch→l is NOT pharma-specific (E≈1.1× pharma enrichment, directional). The formula type operates across sections. Pharma section l-clustering (F423) is due to l-LF and lk-compounds in runs, not d→ch→l routing.
