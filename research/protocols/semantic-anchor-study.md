# Semantic-anchor study (proposal)

Goal: test whether EVA label features predict independently coded illustration categories on held-out folios, beyond section and token-length baselines.

No translation claim. No EXP ID allocated. Do not alter committed claims.

1. Audit the IVTFF parser and label extraction; record provenance and transcription disagreements.
2. Annotate illustration categories blind to EVA transcription, with an annotation guide and disagreement log.
3. Partition by folio before selecting features; prevent duplicate illustrations and labels crossing partitions.
4. Compare a section/length/position baseline against EVA n-gram features, with folio-clustered uncertainty.
5. Freeze primary outcome, holdout, negative controls, seed, and decision rule before evaluation.
6. Interpret any out-of-sample gain as predictive association, not a decipherment.

Pending: corpus inventory, sample sizes, annotation reliability, baseline prevalence, and preregistration.
